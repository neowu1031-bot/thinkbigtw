Think BIG Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

中文版

1. Who is responsible

Think BIG Make it Real CO., LTD. (大想成業有限公司), Taiwan (R.O.C.), Unified Business No. 62136066, No. 167, Wuquan S. Rd., Fuxing Vil., South Dist., Taichung City 402011, Taiwan; AI@thinkbigtw.com (“we”). This Policy covers thinkbigtw.com and our Services. We comply with Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act and, where it applies to you, the EU/UK GDPR (see Section 10).

2. Data we collect

Identity and contact: name, email, phone / WhatsApp handle, company name and business ID. Billing: order and invoice records; card details are handled by our payment provider and we do not store full card numbers. Technical: IP address, browser type, cookies, pages viewed. Enquiry data: what you type into our enquiry forms and AI assistant.

3. Why we use it

To deliver the Services and support you; to reply to enquiries; to process payments and issue invoices; to send you information you asked for (you can opt out at any time); to measure website traffic and improve our services; and to meet legal obligations. Our legal basis under GDPR, where it applies, is mainly performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), plus legitimate interests for site analytics and security, and consent where we ask for it.

You are free to decide whether to give us personal data. What is required, and what happens if you do not provide it: (1) Enquiries, the quick request form and event sign-ups need a name, one contact method and your request; without them we cannot reply or send event notices. (2) Orders need your name, a contact method and the details required for an invoice; without them we cannot accept the order, deliver the Services or issue an invoice as the law requires. (3) Remote installation needs remote access to your device, granted by you; without it we cannot carry out the work (see Section 8 on AI tools). (4) Optional fields, such as company, industry or business ID, can be left blank with no effect on your use of the site or our reply. (5) You can reject or turn off cookies and analytics without affecting your use of the site (see Section 5).

4. Who receives it, and where

We use these service providers: ECPay (payments and e-invoices, Taiwan), Google LLC (analytics, cloud), Cloudflare, Inc. (website, security, Workers AI), Supabase Inc. (database), Meta Platforms, Inc. (ad measurement, only while ads run), and Anthropic PBC and OpenAI (AI tools used for remote work, Section 8), plus Upwork, Fiverr, Wise, PayPal, Payoneer or your bank when you pay or are hired through them. Data may be processed in Taiwan and in the United States and other countries where these providers operate. We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with others except as described here or as required by law.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits, pages, time on page and conversions (for example, an enquiry form sent). You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking third-party cookies. While we run ads, we use the Meta Pixel to measure ad clicks and conversions; you can manage this in Meta ad preferences. The Pixel does not load when no ads are running.

6. Our website AI assistant and enquiry summaries

Our website chat and the “AI Experience” demo send what you type to a third-party cloud AI model (for the demo, Cloudflare Workers AI, after you tick consent) to generate a reply. Please do not enter passwords, API keys, payment details, confidential information or contact details in the chat. We do not store chat transcripts or IP addresses in our enquiry database. After a conversation, an anonymous summary (no name or direct contact data) may be sent to our internal system to improve service. If you choose to send an enquiry, you first review and edit the summary and contact details, tick consent and press Send; only then is it saved in our enquiry database. We keep enquiry summaries and the contact details you gave until you ask us to delete them. We never add you to marketing lists automatically. Messaging apps temporarily hold recent conversation context to keep replies coherent, then clear it after about 30 minutes of inactivity (at most 2 hours).

Enquiry form submissions are processed with AI assistance; the AI service provider for enquiry handling is Anthropic PBC (USA). MiniMax and OpenAI services are used for internal notifications and collaboration. Please do not include passwords, keys, payment details or confidential documents in your submission.

7. Security

We use TLS encryption in transit, access controls and least-privilege permissions. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we learn that personal data we hold has been stolen, altered, damaged, lost or leaked, we will act promptly to contain it and notify the people affected by email or another suitable means, explaining what happened, what we have done and how to reach us with questions. Where the law requires us to report to an authority, we will do so, and we keep records of the incident and our response.

9. How long we keep data

Contact data, enquiry summaries and project records: until you ask us to delete them, or until the purpose ends, whichever is earlier; invoices and accounting records are kept as Taiwan law requires. Deletion requests are handled within 14 days.

10. Your rights

You can ask to access, copy, correct, restrict or delete your personal data, or to stop our use of it, by emailing AI@thinkbigtw.com or via WhatsApp +886 908 898 903. We reply within 14 days. If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the right to data portability and to complain to your local data protection authority. For personal data in your own systems that we work on, you are the controller and we act as processor; a data processing agreement is available on request before the statement of work is signed.

11. Threads / Instagram social engagement tool (Meta Platform data)

Think BIG uses an internal tool, the ThinkBIG Threads Reply Console (Meta app: ThinkBIG Social Monitor), to manage how our official account @think_big_ai takes part in conversations on Threads.

  1. Purpose: We search public Threads posts about using AI at work by keyword. Our staff draft replies, and a human reviewer approves each reply before it is published from our account. We publish at most 10 replies per 24 hours. We do not automatically like, follow, repost or send direct messages.
  2. Data we process:
    • Search results (public post text, the author's public username, the post link and the time) are shown only on our staff's screen. We do not store them.
    • If we decide to reply, we keep only the post ID, the post link, the author's public username and our reply text, as a review record.
    • Comments on our own posts and posts that mention our account: we read the comment text and the commenter's public username to notify our staff for follow-up replies. These are retained in our internal work records; you may request deletion under the Deletion requests section below.
  3. Retention: Review records are deleted automatically 30 days after they are created. We do not build profiles of the people whose posts appear in searches. Reply notifications are sent to the messaging apps used by our staff and remain on their devices; these are outside the automatic deletion described above. If you submit a deletion request, we will handle the portions we are able to delete. Links to public posts may be cited in internal research reports to understand market discussion trends; such citations are for internal reference only.
  4. No sharing: We do not sell this data, share it with third parties or use it for advertising. We handle it in line with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies.
  5. Deletion requests: To ask us to delete records related to your Threads account or posts, email AI@thinkbigtw.com with your Threads username. We will delete the records and confirm within 14 days. You can also remove this app's access in your Threads account settings, under Website permissions.

12. Children

Our website is for adults. Use by minors requires a parent or guardian’s consent. Services involving children’s photos (such as custom storybooks) have a separate privacy notice and consent form provided before ordering.
