Frequently asked questions

Answers on pricing, delivery with AI tools, your files, tax, refunds and data.

Pricing

How are enterprise projects priced?

The Business plan starts from NT$52,600 (excl. tax; NT$55,230 incl. tax; hardware not included) and is quoted to your needs. Book a needs call for a quote. Payment methods and installments are as stated in your quote. AI model usage fees are paid by you directly to the model provider.

Fixed-price services

What is on the fixed-price menu for overseas clients?

Current fixed-price services are the GEO (AI search visibility) Health Check and the FAQ / RAG Support Bot. Packages and what each includes are listed on the pricing page. Anything else is quoted individually.

How do I contact you?

WhatsApp +886 908 898 903, or email AI@thinkbigtw.com. We reply within 3 working days, usually sooner. We do not use LINE for overseas clients.

Working hours

What time zone do you work in?

Taiwan Standard Time (UTC+8). Work is done remotely and in writing; we do not offer on-site visits, video or phone support.

How we deliver

How do you deliver? Do you use AI?

Yes. Our engineering team is AI agents. We deliver with AI tools (Anthropic Claude Code and OpenAI Codex), operated by our staff, who are responsible for the results. Commands and their output are sent to these providers; your documents, conversations, photos, passwords and keys are not. Masking is applied before sending. This is the only way we deliver, so please do not order if you do not accept it.

See the Privacy Policy, Section 8 ›

Your files

Will the AI see my files?

No. We work with installation and configuration commands and their results. We do not open or send your documents, chats or photos. You type your own passwords and keys on your own machine.

Local AI

Can I run it on my own computer (local AI)?

Often yes. Apple Silicon Macs, Windows or Linux with an NVIDIA GPU, and AMD Ryzen AI Max machines can be assessed; send your specs before ordering. If your machine is too small, we use a cloud model instead, under the same plan. Local does not mean "nothing leaves the machine": cloud models, messaging apps and external services still send requests.

Costs

Who pays for AI model usage and third-party accounts?

You. Cloud-model API keys and other third-party accounts (hosting, messaging and similar) are opened by you in your own name; we only guide you. Local models carry no usage fee from us.

Delivery time

How long does delivery take?

For standard packages, within 30 days of receiving everything we need from you. Larger projects follow the timeline in the quote.

Marketplaces

What if my project goes through Upwork or Fiverr?

Payment, escrow, refunds and disputes stay on the platform. We never invoice or accept payment outside it.

Tax and invoices

Do you charge tax? What invoice do I get?

If the service is used outside Taiwan, it is zero-rated for Taiwan business tax (0%) and you receive an English commercial invoice. If it is used in Taiwan, 5% business tax applies. Taxes in your own country are yours.

See the Terms, Section 4 ›

Refunds

What is your refund policy?

Before work starts, you can cancel for a full refund with no deduction. For personal setup, we send a written specification and start only after you confirm in writing. After work starts, we refund in proportion to the steps not completed. If we cannot finish because of our own technical problem, you get a full refund. Refunds are paid within 15 days of your request.

See the Terms, Section 6 ›

After the job

What happens to my data and my machine when the job ends?

At the end of the support period we remove our remote access. If you cancel or get a refund, we remove what we installed, free of charge. We do not delete your own files. Work logs are kept only as long as the service requires; you can ask us to delete them at any time.

EU and UK

I am in the EU or UK. Can you work with me?

Yes, with limits. Personal data in your systems is processed only on your own machine and is never sent to a cloud AI service; only masked commands and output with no personal data may use AI tools. A data processing agreement is available on request before signing.

Your data

How do I ask you to delete my data?

Email AI@thinkbigtw.com or WhatsApp +886 908 898 903. We reply within 14 days.

See the Privacy Policy, Section 10 ›

Security

How do you handle security?

We scan skills before installing them, pin versions with no auto-updates, run a security review before go-live, and keep keys in system configuration files and never in chat. No one can guarantee security; we say so plainly.
