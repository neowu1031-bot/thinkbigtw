Annual Plan — Premier

Your dedicated engineer for the full year.

Annual plan includes operations: proactive fault detection + 48-hour repair SLA
Renewal option available for continued year-over-year maintenance

From setup to long-term operations — one plan covers everything. Fully custom AI Agent architecture (OpenClaw or Hermes, or dual-system MCP sync) with a dedicated engineer for the full year. No need to learn maintenance yourself, and no extra charge every time something breaks — it's already included in the annual plan.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • Your AI tools were set up, then sat unused after the first month — nobody kept adding features
  • When something breaks, you're stuck — and finding someone to fix it costs extra
  • You know AI should help you, but you're not sure which part of your work to automate first
  • You don't want to re-research tools every quarter — you want someone to keep you up to date

The Annual Plan — Premier is designed around the two most common problems: "can't get it working" and "broke, and nobody cares" — both included in one annual fee.
