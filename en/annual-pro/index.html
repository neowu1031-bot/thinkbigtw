Example features, set up to your needs

These are example features. What you get is decided after an interview and an assessment of your device and environment; not every plan includes every feature.

Everything in the Single Agent can be set up to your needs, and we can also build new skills each week based on how you use it (date calculation, reminders, to-do summaries, document summaries, audio to transcript, polishing and proofreading, web lookups and more; see the Single AI Agent page)

Maintenance: weekly routine

  • Weekly health check, fixes as soon as problems are found, plain-language weekly report
  • Self-recovery after disconnection, AI account quota guard, scheduled-delivery sentinel, messaging sign-out detection
  • Backup integrity checks and cleanup, disk and power trend alerts
  • Version upgrade assessment and rollback, usage review

Documents and memory

  • A memory notebook you can see (the Agent's memory and maintenance records can be opened by you)

Communication

  • Customer service reply drafts and templates (subject to environment check)

What you receive

  • A tutorial video made for your setup, with narration: recorded around your actual configuration, showing you how to use it day to day.
  • A PDF user manual: step-by-step operation and common questions, with a "Follow us" page at the end (LINE, Instagram, Facebook, website).

How it is delivered

  • We interview you about your needs before you order, and start the annual plan only once we confirm it will work for you.
  • Installed remotely by an engineer; technical consulting is available during the year.
  • Built mainly for Apple silicon Macs; other devices can be assessed.
  • Limitation: your computer must stay on and not go to sleep. Health checks, backups and reminders only run while the computer is online.
  • Faults are repaired within 48 hours. In the second year you can choose to renew at NT$35,910.

About your data

With a cloud model, your conversations are sent to the model provider for processing; before signing we tell you which provider is used and its data terms. With a local model, your data stays on your own computer.

If it's legal and you can think of it, we'll do our best to build it. For anything beyond the plan's scope, we'll walk you through feasibility and cost during the consultation.
