About Think BIG

Think BIG (Think BIG Make it Real CO., LTD.) is a Taiwan-based enterprise AI agent deployment company serving clients worldwide.

Who we are

Think BIG (Think BIG Make it Real CO., LTD.) is an enterprise AI Agent deployment provider headquartered in Taiwan and serving clients worldwide. We start from keeping company data secure and build AI Agents inside the company's own environment (company equipment, or a cloud host registered to the company) with TB FRAME, our four-phase deployment method (assess, build, validate, operate), plus one year of hands-on maintenance. With the on-premises option, models and documents are processed on company equipment; with the cloud-assisted option, questions and retrieved document passages are sent to the model provider. We act as a remote FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) team for the company: from understanding the need, building the knowledge base and AI Agents, and going live, to ongoing operations, handled end to end. Personal AI assistant setup plans are also available.

On-premises does not mean every connection stays on the machine. If cloud models or external services are used, those requests still leave the device. When data must stay inside the company, local models, outbound restrictions and available features need to be confirmed first.

Company and scope of service

Legal name: Think BIG Make it Real CO., LTD. (in Chinese, 大想成業有限公司), Taiwan Business ID 62136066, headquartered in Taiwan and serving clients worldwide. Services include enterprise AI Agent deployment, workflow integration, data and permission design, scenario acceptance, and annual governance and operations. Personal AI assistant setup and maintenance plans are also offered.

Leadership and team

Think BIG is led by NEO, the company's Founder. Our engineering team is a group of AI agents built on frontier models, and every delivery goes through a security review before it goes live.

The agents carry out building, testing and operations; scope, approvals and accountability stay with people.

Delivery managed with TB FRAME

Assess (define), Build (architecture and build), Validate (acceptance and handover) and Operate (governance and operations) are the four phases of our method. TB READINESS (a pre-deployment self-assessment that confirms workflows, data and preparation) gives a first read; TB DELIVERY (building, testing and delivering the AI workflow within the confirmed scope) covers the build; TB CONTINUITY (one year of maintenance, issue tracking and usage adjustments after deployment) carries on into annual governance and operations. Enterprise services are quoted individually for the confirmed scope; all three plans are delivered within 30 days once scope is frozen and data is in place.

Governance principles

Data and operations are scoped by least privilege. Outbound sending, spending and changes to production systems are approved by a named owner, and every change records its reason, scope, rollback method and verification. We do not publish client names, logos, screenshots or descriptions that could identify a client. Capability statements reflect how the Think BIG team actually operates and governs its work, and do not represent third-party security certification.
