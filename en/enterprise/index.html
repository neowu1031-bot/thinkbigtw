ABOUT THINK BIG

Think BIG (大想成業有限公司) is a Taiwan-based enterprise AI Agent deployment provider serving clients worldwide. Starting from enterprise data security, we use TB FRAME — our four-phase deployment methodology: Assess, Build, Validate, Operate — to build AI Agents inside your own environment (your hardware, or a cloud host registered to your account). We provide one year of run-alongside operations support. With on-premises deployment, the model and your documents are processed on your company hardware. With cloud-assisted deployment, questions and retrieved document passages are sent to the model provider under their terms of service. We act as your outsourced FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) team: from understanding requirements, building the knowledge base and AI Agents, and going live, through to ongoing operations.


On-premises deployment does not mean all connections are local. If a cloud model or external service is used, related requests are still sent out. If data must not leave your premises, local model support, outbound restrictions, logging and backup configuration must each be confirmed.

DATA SOVEREIGNTY

Choose your deployment architecture
based on your data policy.

Data sovereignty (Data Sovereignty) is enforced through processing location, access permissions and outbound transmission rules. Select the configuration based on your enterprise policy and hardware conditions, then verify connector compatibility item by item.

On-premises

The model and designated data are processed on your company hardware. If data must not leave your premises, logging, backup and all outbound connections must also be verified.

Cloud-assisted

When a cloud model is used, questions and retrieved document passages are sent to the model provider for processing under their terms of service. Before deployment, list the fields sent, purposes, account permissions and costs, then confirm authorisation before including in the workflow.

Supported technologies: OpenClaw, Claude, MCP, RAG. Integration and model changes require compatibility confirmation and re-testing of work results.

PROCUREMENT FAQ

Clarify deployment conditions
to support enterprise purchasing decisions.

From data handling and service boundaries to operations responsibility — the foundational information needed for deployment assessment and procurement discussions.

How should we evaluate an enterprise AI deployment provider?

Evaluation should cover workflow fit, data governance, delivery acceptance and ongoing operations. Ask providers to list scope, access permissions, acceptance criteria and incident responsibility. Think BIG demonstrates capability through a live AI team governance process; no client identities or data are shown.

How does on-premises deployment enforce data sovereignty?

On-premises configuration deploys work data and the execution environment to the client's designated hardware. When cloud models or external services are used, the fields sent, purposes and authorisation must be confirmed separately. Requiring data to stay on-site still needs verification of local model support, outbound restrictions, logging and backup configuration. With the cloud-assisted option, questions and retrieved document passages are sent to the model provider under their terms of service.

Does deployment suitability depend on company size?

Suitability depends primarily on how well-defined the target process is, data availability and acceptance responsibility. A single workflow can be scoped for assessment first, then expanded based on validated results. Personal-use requirements are covered by the personal plans.

How is deployment progress planned and managed?

TB FRAME manages deployment in four phases: Assess, Build, Validate and Operate. Progress is confirmed jointly based on scope, data readiness, system access and acceptance criteria, tracked through phase deliverables. All three plans deliver within 30 days of scope freeze and data handover.

What plans are available for enterprise deployment?

Enterprise plans are priced per the quotation. Please request a requirements consultation to receive a formal quote. All three plans (Starter, Standard, Complete) include build, tutorial videos and first-year maintenance. Any plan can be upgraded within Year 1 by paying the price difference. Year-2 renewal is optional — decided by the client, with no automatic billing.

How is enterprise service scope defined for quotation?

All three plans have clearly defined scopes and are not individually re-priced. Requirements outside plan scope (e.g. auto-outbound replies to customers, ERP write-back, large-scale integrations) are handled as separate engagements. Deployment service and AI model costs are itemised separately for client purchasing clarity.

What operations responsibilities does TB CONTINUITY cover?

TB CONTINUITY | AI Agent Annual Governance and Operations provides one year of run-alongside support, covering incident tracking, maintenance checks, change records and usage adjustments within the agreed scope. Service contact, responsibility boundaries and new-requirement handling are confirmed in the contract.

How is the AI Agent's autonomous operation scope controlled?

Data and operation scope are configured following least-privilege principles. Outbound sends, spend and formal system changes require approval by the designated responsible person. The AI Agent produces work products and execution evidence; people retain final decision authority.

Can existing tools and models continue to be used?

Existing system interfaces, account permissions and compatibility are inventoried before integration scope is assessed. Changing models requires re-testing workflows and output quality; whether existing configurations can be reused is confirmed through actual testing.
