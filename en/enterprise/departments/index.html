FUNCTIONAL AI AGENTS

Collaborate by function.
Reshape your workflows.

Configure AI Agents by customer service, sales, marketing, finance and operations function, integrating data inputs, deliverables and human decision points into a clear operating model. The capability ranges below are examples for assessment, not client case studies.

Customer serviceSales and outreachMarketingFinanceOperations and security

CUSTOMER SERVICE AI AGENT SET

Support customer service with a knowledge foundation.

Capability scope
Research questions and prepare replies based on approved knowledge sources.
Deliverables
Reply drafts with cited sources; list of questions that cannot be answered.
Human decisions and authorization
Escalate to staff when no source applies; commitments, refunds and disputes are decided by the responsible person.

How is knowledge prepared for enterprise Q&A? Learn about RAG, document permissions and acceptance testing ›

SALES AND OUTREACH AI AGENT SET

Integrate enquiry records and outreach tracking.

Capability scope
Organize enquiries, contact reasons and follow-up tasks; prepare outreach drafts following agreed workflows.
Deliverables
Enquiry summaries, pending contact lists and status per item.
Human decisions and authorization
Confirm outreach authorization, channel rules and opt-out mechanisms before sending; no auto-dial or auto-send is assumed.

MARKETING AI AGENT SET

Build a consistent content production and review workflow.

Capability scope
Compile approved brand materials into drafts; check content and flag items to be filled in.
Deliverables
Editable content drafts, sources and a pre-publish checklist.
Human decisions and authorization
Publishing, ad placements and any spend require human approval.

FINANCE AI AGENT SET

Strengthen data consolidation and variance reconciliation.

Capability scope
Organize data to agreed formats; flag missing items and entries that need manual review.
Deliverables
Consolidated tables, variance notes and traceable data sources.
Human decisions and authorization
Payments, tax filings and financial judgements are made by the designated person; AI output is not executed directly.

OPERATIONS AND SECURITY AI AGENT SET

Build a traceable operations management workflow.

Capability scope
Run routine checks within the approved scope; link anomalies, changes and verification results into a pending queue.
Deliverables
Inspection summaries, change logs, anomaly and recovery checklists.
Human decisions and authorization
Register formal system changes in advance; open permissions only as needed; the guard verifies evidence before confirming completion.

VALIDATE BEFORE SCALE

Assess scale-up based on validated results.

Start by defining data inputs, deliverable specifications, review responsibility and prohibited operations; then confirm scope through scenario-based acceptance testing. Cross-department scale-up builds on validated results and operational capacity from existing workflows.
