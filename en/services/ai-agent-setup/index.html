AI Agent setup: your AI assistant, installed on your own computer or company server

AI Agent setup means installing an AI assistant that can read your materials and complete tasks by your rules on your own computer, your company server, or a cloud server registered to your company, and connecting it to the LINE, Telegram, or internal tools you already use. Think BIG (大想成業有限公司) is an AI Agent setup and deployment team based in Taiwan. We work with individuals and businesses, and our Taiwan-based engineers install remotely.

08FAQ

What is AI Agent setup?

We install an AI assistant that can read your materials and complete tasks by your rules on your own computer, your company server, or a cloud server registered to your company, then connect it to LINE, Telegram, or internal tools. Setup covers installation, integration, permission settings, and acceptance testing.

How is the cost of AI Agent setup calculated?

The cost is made up of a setup service fee, hardware, third-party software, and AI model usage fees. The on-premises version has no separate model usage fee; the cloud-assisted version is billed separately by the model provider based on usage. Personal plans can be purchased on our Shopee store; business projects are quoted by workflow scope and deployment type.

How is personal setup different from business setup?

Personal setup usually means one computer and one AI assistant, typically connected to LINE or Telegram. Business setup starts by confirming department workflows, data sources, access permissions, and acceptance criteria, then choosing the on-premises or cloud-assisted version.

Do I need a powerful computer?

The on-premises version needs a graphics card and memory that match the model's requirements; if your hardware falls short, the cloud-assisted version is an option. Our engineers check your hardware specs before recommending an approach. See our hardware sizing guide for local models first.

Will my data be sent out?

With the on-premises version, the model and documents are processed on your own device. With the cloud-assisted version, questions and retrieved document passages are sent to the model provider. Requests to external services are still sent when those services are used; if data must stay inside your company, local models and outbound limits are confirmed together.

Can it work as a LINE Official Account customer service assistant?

Yes. An AI Agent can be connected to your LINE Official Account and answer inquiries based on documents you provide. We recommend a person reviews outbound replies before they are sent. LINE Official Account message fees are charged separately according to LINE's published pricing.

How does remote installation work? Is it safe?

You open a remote connection on your computer and our engineer connects to install; you can watch the whole process. Please do not send passwords or API keys to us by message. The connection method and what happens after the session are explained before installation.

How do I get started?

Send us a sentence or two on WhatsApp about the work you want to hand to AI, plus your computer or server specs. Business inquiries can also use the 2-minute request form, and an engineer will reply after confirming the scope.
