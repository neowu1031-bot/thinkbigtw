Two channels, one foundation

Two ways customers find you — both need to work

Today's customers find you in two very different ways. Both rely on the same underlying foundation, so doing them separately is wasteful — doing them together is efficient.

Channel 1: Google Search (SEO)

When a customer types your industry and location into Google, where do you rank?

  • How many of your pages Google has indexed, which important pages are missing
  • Mobile usability, page speed, and technical architecture issues
  • Title tags, descriptions, and structured data correctness
  • Local search and Google Business Profile completeness
  • Where you currently rank for the keywords your customers actually search

Channel 2: AI Answers (GEO)

A growing share of customers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI directly: "Which company in this category is recommended?"

  • Whether 5 major AI platforms mention you, and how they describe you
  • Which sources the AI cites, and how competitors compare
  • Whether the AI has described you incorrectly (wrong address, missing services, confusion with another brand)
  • Whether the AI answered from training memory or actually retrieved your site
  • Different AI platforms prefer entirely different sources — what works for one may not work for another
Both channels rely on the same foundation: a crawlable site, well-structured content, a consistent identity across platforms, and credible off-site mentions. We address all of this together — no duplicated work, no conflicting advice.

Methodology

Why we measure this way

The measurement method determines whether a report is worth trusting. We publish the methodology because transparency is the core of this service.

Repeated sampling, not a single query

Academic research (University of St. Gallen, 2026) has confirmed that re-running the same question on the same day yields citations with only a 32–43% overlap. AI citation is a probability distribution — a single query is statistically near-meaningless. We query each question across multiple days, then review results manually to derive a defensible range.

Per-AI breakdown, not a universal list

Taiwan field data shows: Gemini tends to cite official websites and review directories; Perplexity favors Threads and YouTube discussions; ChatGPT favors government institution directories and cites non-Chinese sources 70% of the time. Cross-platform source overlap is only 5–20%. Your action list is broken down by AI platform.

Confidence intervals, not single-point numbers

For a brand detection rate to have a standard error below 0.10, you need at least 7 repetitions on the same day and a rolling window of 10+ days. Our reports only make claims supported by statistical evidence: if confidence intervals overlap, we write "no meaningful change," not a fabricated improvement figure.

Retrieval trigger rate: memory vs. live retrieval

57% of ChatGPT responses to queries don't trigger a web search at all — the model answers from training memory, meaning your website is invisible to it regardless of how good it is. We log the trigger status per question; the two gap types require entirely different fixes.

Methodology you can verify yourself

Every report includes: which questions were asked, on which dates, with which AI version, how many repetitions, API or browser, and region settings. You can take the same questions and re-run them yourself to verify our numbers aren't fabricated.

SEO measurement: index, technical, keywords, local

  • Index: site: queries and Search Console index status
  • Technical: Core Web Vitals public data, mobile, HTTPS, canonicals
  • Keywords: Search Console or manual queries, factual current-state report only
  • Local: Google Business Profile completeness, map ranking position

What we never do

Our hard limits — stated plainly

These practices are available in the market. We don't use them — not because we can't, but because they harm you.

✕

Buying clicks or traffic

Artificially inflating clicks or purchasing traffic violates Google's spam policies and can result in your site being penalized or removed. We don't do it and don't recommend it.

✕

Buying links or building PBNs

Link buying and private blog networks are explicitly cited violations in Google's spam policies. They may produce short-term effects but carry serious long-term risk. We only build genuine, sustainable off-site presence.

✕

Posting commercial content on forums that prohibit it

Forums like Dcard and PTT explicitly prohibit commercial promotion in their rules. Getting caught means deleted posts, account bans, and reputational damage. We only produce named, compliant off-site content.

✕

Fake reviews, sock-puppet accounts, anonymous astroturfing

Taiwan's Fair Trade Act prohibits content written in a third-party voice to mislead consumers. Any paid recommendation we produce is disclosed. We don't write fake reviews or maintain fake accounts.

✕

Manipulating AI answers (hidden text, prompt injection)

Google explicitly listed "manipulating AI-generated answers" in its spam policies in 2026. Hidden text and prompt injection can get your site penalized or removed. Our approach is to make content genuinely worth citing — not to deceive the system.

✕

Promising specific rankings or citation counts

Rankings are decided by Google. AI citations are probability distributions. Anyone who tells you "first page guaranteed" or "AI will definitely recommend you" is telling you what you want to hear. We give you real data, not empty promises.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between SEO and GEO, and why do them together?
SEO is about being found when someone searches on Google. GEO is about being cited when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or a similar AI a question. Both rely on the same foundation: a crawlable site, well-structured content, consistent identity across platforms, and credible off-site mentions. Doing them separately means duplicated effort — we audit and prioritize both together in one pass.
I don't have a website. Can I still use this service?
Yes. For businesses without a website, we audit your Google Business Profile, marketplace listings, directories, and social channels instead. The AI visibility check works identically. For the foundation build, we create off-site content assets rather than modifying a website.
Can you get me to the first page of Google?
Rankings are determined by Google — no one can promise you a specific position. We do not buy clicks, traffic, or links; those violate Google's spam policies. What we commit to is doing the right work and providing verifiable monthly data so you can see your search and AI visibility develop over time.
How long before I see results?
SEO and GEO both accumulate — they are not on/off switches. In general: initial changes from content restructuring appear within 4–8 weeks; AI citations start to appear around months 5–6; noticeable presence growth comes around months 6–12. Actual timelines depend on industry competitiveness, how much verifiable information you publish, and AI platform algorithm changes. We do not commit to specific rankings or dates.
How do you measure AI visibility? Is it just a screenshot of one query?
No. We query five major AI platforms with 15–20 questions your customers would actually ask, repeated across multiple days. Academic research (University of St. Gallen, 2026) shows citation overlap between runs on the same question on the same day is only 32–43% — a single query is statistically near-meaningless. Our reports show a range with a confidence interval. Every report includes the full methodology — which questions, which day, which AI version, how many repetitions — so you can re-run the queries yourself and verify.
What do you never do?
We never buy clicks, traffic, or links; never create fake reviews or sock-puppet accounts; never post commercial content on forums that prohibit it; and never use hidden text or prompt injection to influence AI answers. Google explicitly listed AI answer manipulation in its spam policies in 2026. We build genuinely citable content — not gaming the system.
What do I need to provide to get started?
Three things: your industry, your brand name or URL (if you don't have a website, tell us where you sell), and the 3–5 competitors you care most about. We'll draft 15–20 questions your customers would actually ask an AI, share them with you first, then run the full check. For the Google search audit, Search Console read access makes the report significantly more complete — but it works without it too.
Is my information kept confidential?
Your brand information, competitor list, and any Search Console data you authorize us to read are used solely to produce your report. They are not used for other client research, not shared externally, and not used as marketing material. Client identities and cases are always kept confidential — we never name clients publicly. That is a baseline commitment.
