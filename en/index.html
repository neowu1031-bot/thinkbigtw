ABOUT THINK BIG

An enterprise AI Agent deployment team, based in Taiwan.

Think BIG (Think BIG Make it Real CO., LTD.) is an enterprise AI Agent deployment provider headquartered in Taiwan and serving clients worldwide. Starting from security, we build AI Agents inside the company's own environment (company equipment, or a cloud host registered to the company) with TB FRAME, our four-phase deployment method (assess, build, validate, operate), and provide one year of hands-on maintenance. With the on-premises option, models and documents are processed on company equipment; with the cloud-assisted option, questions and retrieved document passages are sent to the model provider. We act as a remote FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) team for the company: from understanding the need, building the knowledge base and AI Agents, and going live, to ongoing operations, handled end to end. Personal AI assistant setup plans are also available.

On-premises does not mean every connection stays on the machine. If cloud models or external services are used, those requests still leave the device. When data must stay inside the company, local models, outbound restrictions and available features need to be confirmed first.

GOVERNANCE

Least privilege, human approval, no named clients.

Data and operations are scoped by least privilege. Outbound sending, spending and changes to production systems are approved by a named owner, and every change records its reason, scope, rollback method and verification. We do not publish client names, logos, screenshots or descriptions that could identify a client. Capability statements reflect how the Think BIG team actually operates and governs its work, and do not represent third-party security certification.
