ASSESSMENT / Recommendations

  • Map workflow steps and exception cases.
  • List usable data, sources and who authorizes their use.
  • Name an acceptance owner and a change approver.
  • Define where data is processed and what may be sent externally.
  • Specify output formats, prohibited actions and acceptance scenarios.
  • Prepare cleared work samples and set the pilot scope using normal and exception cases.
  • Assume a fully on-premises setup and check whether hardware and workflows can keep data inside.
  • Confirm each field sent to external services and its purpose; add them to the workflow only after approval.

This is a conditional, preliminary self-assessment. It is not a benefit forecast, a certification or a formal advisory assessment.

ENTERPRISE ADVISORY

Clarify procurement criteria. Ground the decision.

Choosing a deployment partner? Start with our vendor selection guide.

What does a company need before deployment?

No in-house AI development capability is required. The assessment starts from workflows, data cleared for use, an acceptance owner and expected outputs, then confirms where AI Agents fit and what preparation is needed.

How does on-premises deployment define the data processing scope?

In a fully on-premises setup, models and designated data are processed on company hardware. If a workflow needs external models or services, the fields sent, their purpose and authorization are confirmed first. On-premises deployment does not mean no integration ever goes online.

How are operations and governance maintained after deployment?

TB CONTINUITY (one year of post-deployment maintenance, issue tracking and usage adjustments) provides annual governance and operations: one year of hands-on support with issue tracking, maintenance checks and change verification within the agreed scope. Hardware, model usage, third-party services and new requirements are scoped separately.
