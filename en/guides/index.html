AI Implementation Decisions
Start with a Clear Evaluation Framework.

Think BIG is a Taiwan-based enterprise AI Agent deployment firm serving clients globally. We start from enterprise data security and deploy AI Agents inside your company's own environment — using TB FRAME (our four-stage deployment methodology: Assess, Build, Validate, Operate) on company equipment or company-owned cloud infrastructure — with one year of ongoing operations support. On-premises option: models and documents are processed entirely on company equipment. Cloud-assisted option: queries and retrieved document segments are sent to the model provider for processing. We act as your company's remote FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) team: from understanding requirements, building knowledge bases and AI Agents, and going live, to ongoing operations — end to end. Personal AI assistant installation plans are also available.

On-premises deployment does not mean all connections are local. When cloud models or external services are used, relevant requests are still transmitted. When data must stay entirely in-house, confirm local model selection, outbound restrictions, and available functionality.
