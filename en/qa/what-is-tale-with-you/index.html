What Is Tale With You? An AI Custom Picture Book Where Your Child Is the Main Character

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-19   ·  中文

The short answer

Tale With You (童話有你) is an AI-customized picture book where your child is the main character. Parents upload 1–3 frontal photos; the child's face and name are drawn into the title and every page of the story by AI. Available in 10 themes (from birthday adventures to dinosaur expeditions), two illustration styles, Chinese text with zhuyin phonetic notation on every character, optional English sentences. The book is 21×21 cm, 26 interior pages, NT$1,500 including domestic Taiwan shipping, approximately 4 working days to produce after the preview is confirmed. Orders are placed on Shopee. Operated by 大想成業有限公司 (Think BIG Make it Real CO., LTD.), unified number 62136066.

2. Can parents be drawn in too?

Yes—one or two adults (parents, grandparents) may also appear in the book. Each adult must personally provide consent and 1–2 frontal photos. No extra charge; full details in Can Parents Be Drawn Into a Custom Book Too?

3. What happens after you order

  1. Place the order on Shopee and complete payment.
  2. Send the child's name and 1–3 frontal photos via Shopee chat; sign the photo consent form.
  3. The team produces a preview (cover and partial pages, low-resolution, with watermark)—usually within the stated production time.
  4. Review the preview: unlimited text edits; one free full regeneration if the likeness is off.
  5. Confirm the preview; printing begins. No content changes are possible after this point.
  6. The finished book ships; estimated delivery time per the logistics provider.
  7. All photos and production files are deleted within 3 days of confirmed delivery.

4. The 10 current themes

Themes include: Birthday Adventure, Taiwan Island Tour, Taiwan Festival Fun, Dinosaur Age, Dream Careers, Becoming a Big Sibling, Emotion Planet, World Tour, Vehicles Extravaganza, and Goodnight Sleepwalk. Each theme is available in both illustration styles.

FAQ

Q1: What is Tale With You?
Tale With You is an AI-customized picture book where your child is the main character. Parents upload 1–3 frontal photos; the child's face and name appear in the title and throughout the 26-page story. Available in 10 themes, two illustration styles, NT$1,500 including domestic Taiwan shipping, ~4 working days to produce.
Q2: Where do I order a Tale With You book?
Orders are placed on Shopee (shopee.tw/talewithyoutw). The official website does not accept direct orders.
Q3: Can I request a preview before the book is printed?
Yes. Every order includes a preview (cover and partial pages, low-resolution with watermark) before printing begins. You may request unlimited text edits and one free full regeneration before confirming.
Q4: How long does production take?
Approximately 4 working days after you confirm the preview and payment is complete. This excludes Saturday, Sunday, and public holidays. It does not start from the order date—photo submission and preview exchange happen first.
Q5: How are my child's photos protected?
Photos are used only to make this book, not for AI training. They are stored with AES-256 encryption and deleted within 3 days of confirmed delivery. Full details at talewithyoutw.com/privacy.html.
