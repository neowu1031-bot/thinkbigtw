What Is Tale With You? An AI Custom Picture Book Where Your Child Is the Main Character

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-19   ·  中文

The short answer

Tale With You is a custom AI picture book where the child is the main character: parents upload 1–3 front-facing photos of the child; based on the photos, the child is illustrated in the story, and the child's name appears in the book title and in the story. Each book is 21 × 21 cm, 26 interior pages, Chinese with Zhuyin (English optional); there are currently 10 themes; NT$1,500 per book, including shipping within Taiwan; approximately 4 working days to produce then shipped. Orders are not accepted on the website itself — please order on Shopee. Operated by Think BIG Make it Real Co., Ltd.

1. How it differs from an ordinary picture book

An ordinary picture book's main character is a character set by the book; in Tale With You, the main character is illustrated based on the child's photos, the child's name appears in the book title and story, making a book that belongs to that one child. Boys and girls can both have one made.

2. What is in the book

  • 10 themes: Taiwan Island Tour, World Tour, Dinosaur Age, Dream Career, Vehicles, Taiwan Festivals, Birthday, Becoming a Big Sibling, Bedtime, and Emotion Planet.
  • Two art styles, chosen at the time of ordering: Style A (watercolor album) has a soft, hand-painted watercolor feel; Style B (toy-figure 3D) has a three-dimensional toy model feel.
  • The puppy Tangyuan: Every book has Tangyuan as a companion — a creamy white Taiwan mixed-breed puppy, one ear caramel-colored, wearing a red collar.
  • Text: Every Chinese character has Zhuyin alongside it so the child can read it themselves; if the English option is chosen, each page also has one rhyming short sentence — not a word-for-word translation.
  • Parents can appear too: Submitting your own photos is optional and at no extra charge. 1–2 adults, 1–2 solo front-facing photos per adult; the adult will appear as an AI illustration in the book, but the main character is still the child — and the person in the photos must give their own consent.

3. How to order

  1. Place an order and pay on Shopee (the website currently does not accept orders or photos directly).
  2. After payment, receive the photo usage instructions in Shopee chat; submit 1–3 front-facing photos of the child only after agreeing: good lighting, clear face, only the child in the photo — a phone photo is fine.
  3. Approximately 4 working days to produce, then shipped; a preview will be sent for your confirmation before printing.

4. How photos are handled

Photos are used only to produce this one book; they are not used in advertisements or on social media, and are not used to train AI. During production, photos are processed by OpenAI's AI image service, with "improve the model with data" disabled on the account. Within 3 days of the book's delivery (confirmed by the logistics delivery record), all files for this order are deleted; for orders with no delivery record, photos are automatically deleted 60 days after upload. Data OpenAI retains under its own policy is outside this deletion scope; see the Privacy Policy on the website for details. Because files are deleted, reprinting after delivery requires re-uploading photos; reprinting due to print defects is free.

For other questions to ask a provider before ordering, see How is privacy protected when using a child's photo for a custom picture book?

5. Limitations to know in advance

The illustration is drawn by AI based on the photo and may not exactly match the child's appearance, so view the preview before printing. All characters in books and videos are AI-generated; the representative figures on the website (including family images) are also AI-generated, not real people.

FAQ

Q1: How much does a Tale With You book cost and how long does it take?
NT$1,500 per book, including shipping within Taiwan. Approximately 4 working days to produce, then shipped.
Q2: Can I order directly on the Tale With You website?
Not currently. The website does not accept orders or photos — please order on Shopee; after payment, you will receive photo usage instructions in Shopee chat, and photos are submitted only after you agree.
Q3: Which company operates Tale With You?
Operated by Think BIG Make it Real Co., Ltd. Per the Ministry of Economic Affairs' Business Registration Open Data, the company's unified business number is 62136066, registered authority is Taichung City Government, and the date of approved establishment is 30 June, Year 115 of the Republic of China.
Q4: Will Tale With You keep the child's photos?
No. Within 3 days of the book's delivery, all files for the order are deleted, including photos, production files, preview images, and the finished PDF; for orders with no delivery record, photos are automatically deleted 60 days after upload. Legally required order records do not contain any photos. Data OpenAI retains under its own policy is outside this deletion scope; see the Privacy Policy on the website for details.
