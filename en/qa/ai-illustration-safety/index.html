Shared Reading & Custom Picture Books Q08

Are AI-Generated Picture Book Illustrations Safe? 5 Things Parents Should Understand First

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-18   ·  中文

The short answer

'Safe' depends on what you are evaluating. For illustration safety, AI-generated picture books have no different physical safety concerns from traditionally illustrated ones. The more important questions are about information: does the provider clearly label books as AI-generated, how are the child's photos handled, is a preview given before printing, is the final book human-checked for image consistency, and are demo images on the website made from real children? Taiwan's Basic Act on Artificial Intelligence, Article 4, promulgated January 14, 2026, explicitly lists transparency and explainability as a required principle—AI-generated content should be disclosed or labeled.

1. Taiwan's AI Basic Law: disclosure is required

Taiwan's Basic Act on Artificial Intelligence, Article 4, promulgated January 14, 2026 (Laws & Regulations Database), lists "transparency and explainability" as one of the AI development principles the government and industry should follow. This includes: AI behavior should be traceable, appropriate disclosures should be made, and AI-generated output should be labeled. Article 5 specifically lists "protection of the rights and interests of children and young people" as a principle that must be considered in AI development.

These are principles, not criminal penalties, but they establish clear expectations for responsible providers.

2. 5 things to check before buying an AI custom picture book

1. Is the book labeled as AI-generated?
Reputable providers add a label such as "AI-generated illustrations" on the cover, back cover, or title page so that readers—and the child when older—know the origin of the artwork.

2. Where do the child's photos go, and who handles them?
See Sharing Your Child's Photo for a Custom Picture Book: 6 Privacy Questions to Ask First. The key questions: which companies process the photo, is it used to train AI, how long is it stored, and when is it deleted.

3. Is a preview provided before printing?
A preview lets you see the actual illustrations before the book is printed. This is the chance to catch images where the AI did not capture the child’s likeness well enough and request a regeneration. Without a preview, you have no recourse before printing starts.

4. Is the book human-checked page by page?
AI image generation can produce unexpected results: extra fingers, inconsistent hair color across pages, unclear text. A provider that checks every page by hand before sending the preview reduces the chance of an unsatisfactory book reaching you.

5. Are demo images on the website made from real children?
Demo images and marketing materials made from real children's photos without proper consent are a red flag. A responsible provider uses images generated for demonstration purposes from non-identifying fictional characters, or discloses clearly that demo images use consenting families with their permission.

3. What AI illustration is good at—and not good at

Current AI illustration can consistently render style, background, props, and mood. What it cannot reliably guarantee is an exact likeness of a specific child. The face, hair color, and eye shape will be in the right general direction, but parents should view the preview critically and compare it against the original photo. Free regeneration (one full-book redo per order) is the safety net for cases where the first attempt misses the mark.

FAQ

Q1: Are AI-generated illustrations in picture books safe?
Physical safety is the same as traditionally illustrated books. The important questions are informational: is the book labeled as AI-generated, how are the child's photos handled, is a preview provided, is the book human-checked, and are demo images on the website real children? Taiwan's AI Basic Law Article 4 (Jan 2026) requires transparency and explainability including disclosure of AI-generated output.
Q2: Does Taiwan's AI Basic Law require AI picture books to be labeled?
Taiwan's Basic Act on Artificial Intelligence Article 4 (promulgated January 14, 2026) requires transparency including appropriate disclosure of AI-generated output. This is a principle-based rule, not a criminal penalty; responsible providers follow it by labeling their books.
Q3: How do I know the AI illustration will look like my child?
AI drawing from photos captures general likeness (face shape, hair, skin tone) but may not be exact. This is why a preview before printing is essential.
