How to Choose a Custom Picture Book: 7 Questions to Ask Before You Buy

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-11-04   ·  中文

The short answer

A custom picture book is not a standard product: the same price can buy a mass-produced book with a name printed on the cover or a fully illustrated story where your child's face appears on every page. Before ordering, seven questions help you understand exactly what you are buying: how customized the story actually is, what the physical quality is like, how the child's photos are handled, whether you can review and change things before printing, what the production timeline is, and what happens if something goes wrong.

1. Seven questions to ask

Question 1: What is actually customized?
The difference between "the child's name appears in the text" and "the child's face is drawn into every illustration" is significant. Ask: does the child appear visually in every spread, or only on a personalized name page? Is the story fixed, or can I choose a theme?

Question 2: Can I read the story before ordering?
A reputable provider will show you a sample book or a full story outline so you know what the narrative is before you submit photos. If you cannot read the story first, you are buying a blind product.

Question 3: What are the physical specifications?
Ask: size, page count, paper type, binding method (saddle-stitch vs. sewn or glued perfect binding), and whether the cover is softcover or hardcover. These affect durability and how well the book survives repeated reading by a child.

Question 4: How are photos handled?
See Sharing Your Child's Photo: 6 Privacy Questions to Ask First for the full framework. The minimum to ask: who handles the photos (and do they cross borders), how are they stored, when are they deleted.

Question 5: What is the preview and revision process?
Key sub-questions: When is the preview provided (before or after payment)? Is it the full book or partial pages? Low-resolution or print quality? How many text changes are allowed? How many full regenerations are allowed? What is the timeframe for reviewing?

Question 6: How is the production timeline calculated?
"Production time" can mean different things. Does it start from the order date, from photo submission, or from the preview confirmation? Does it include weekends and public holidays?

Question 7: What is the cancellation and defect policy?
Under Taiwan's Consumer Protection Act, customized goods can be excluded from the 7-day return right if the seller properly notifies you beforehand (see Can I Return a Custom Picture Book?). Ask: when can I cancel for a full refund, what qualifies as a defect, and what is the remedy (refund, reprint, or exchange)?

FAQ

Q1: What should I check before buying a custom picture book?
Seven things: customization scope (does the child appear visually throughout), story content (can you read it before ordering), physical specs (size, page count, binding), photo handling (who handles them, how stored, when deleted), preview and revision rules (what can you change, how many times), production timeline (when does the clock start), and cancellation/defect policy.
Q2: What is the difference between 'name in text' and 'face in illustrations'?
Name-only customization adds the child's name to fixed template text; the child does not appear in the illustrations. Face-in-illustrations means the AI draws the child's actual appearance into each scene. These are very different products at potentially similar prices.
Q3: Is a preview always provided before printing for a custom book?
Not by all providers. Some send the preview only after full payment; some provide only a cover preview, not interior pages. With Tale With You, the preview is provided before printing begins and you can request text edits (unlimited) and one free full regeneration before confirming.
