How to Choose a Custom Picture Book: 7 Questions to Ask Before You Buy

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-11-04   ·  中文

The short answer

When choosing a custom picture book, first ask clearly about 7 things: customization scope, story content, physical book specifications, photo use, preview and revisions, delivery schedule, and cancellation and defect handling. Keep the answers and then decide whether to provide photos and place the order. Sample images help you assess the art style; your own preview is what you use to confirm the name and illustrations.

1. Turn a page you like into specific questions

It is recommended to first pick one sample page you like and ask the provider "which parts will change in the actual book and which won't." The placement of the name, how the character is illustrated, and whether the story can be adjusted can all be asked separately. Rather than writing "I want it to look a lot like the photo," you can also describe what you care about—the fringe, glasses, or nickname—and ask the provider what range they can handle.

Then paste the following checklist into your own notes app. Leave one line to fill in the answer for each item; when the answer is unclear, stay in the inquiry phase. This is an organization method for making a purchase, not a quality rating of any provider, nor does it judge which type of book is more suitable for a child based on the number of customizable items.

2. Ask these 7 questions before ordering

  1. How much can be customized? Only the name, or is the character illustrated based on photos? Can a nickname be used? Can adults appear?
  2. What is the story about? Which samples can be viewed? Is the plot fixed or adjustable? First see whether the child is interested in the subject.
  3. What is the physical book like? How are the size, interior page count, text, paper material, and binding described? Don't treat the art style name as the paper material specification.
  4. Who gets the photos and where are they used? Which services are involved in production? Is cross-border processing involved? How long until deletion, and which records cannot be deleted?
  5. How much of the preview can be seen and what can be revised? Only the cover or some interior pages? What are the rules for text revisions and regeneration? Can changes still be made after confirmation?
  6. When is production, when does it arrive? What is the starting condition—payment, photos submitted, or preview confirmed? Do working days include holidays? How long does shipping take after production?
  7. What happens with cancellations or defects? Is the handling different before and after printing? Who do you contact with issues, and what photos or records are needed?

For item 4, the photo notification can be checked against Personal Data Protection Act Article 8, which covers: the name of the collecting party, the purpose of collection, the category of data, the period, territory, recipients, and methods of use, and how the data subject can exercise their rights. (Laws and Regulations Database) The checklist breaks some items into everyday phrasing, which does not mean every question is a legal requirement listed item by item.

3. Review the terms, then provide photos and confirm the preview

The Regulations Governing Reasonable Exceptions to the Right of Rescission in Distance Transactions Article 2(2) lists "customized products made at the consumer's request," and Article 2 requires the business to notify the consumer before excluding the right of rescission described in that article. (Laws and Regulations Database) Don't skip reading the cancellation rules just because you see the word "custom." This is provided for reference and does not constitute legal advice.

It is recommended to keep the product description, customer service replies, and the preview you confirmed in the same folder. If you don't know what to ask about the photos, read photo privacy for custom picture books; for cancellation questions see returns and exceptions for custom picture books. Read the name and dedication once more before confirming; there is no need to reply immediately.

FAQ

Q1: What should I look at first with a custom picture book?
First look at the story and samples, then ask which content can be customized and which is fixed, then check the photo use and confirmation process.
Q2: Are the sample images the same as my own preview?
These two should be confirmed separately. Samples are for assessing the art style; the preview is for confirming the name and character illustration in your own order.
Q3: Does a custom product mean defects can't be reported?
The right of rescission exception and defect handling are different questions; each should be asked about before purchase; individual disputes need to be judged based on applicable regulations and transaction content.
Q4: Where do I order Tale With You?
According to the official ordering instructions, orders are currently placed on Shopee; the website itself does not accept orders or photos.
