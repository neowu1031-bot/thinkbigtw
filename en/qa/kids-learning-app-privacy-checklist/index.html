Choosing a Language-Learning App for Your Child: 5 Things to Check Before Downloading

Think BIG Q&A Library　·　November 9, 2026　·　中文版

Short answer

Before downloading a language-learning app for a child, check five things: what data it collects from the child, whether parental consent is needed, who receives the data, how long it is kept, and how to request deletion. The US COPPA Rule covers online services directed to children under 13 and requires notice to parents and verifiable parental consent before personal information is collected, and retention only as long as necessary for the purpose. In Taiwan, Article 8 of the Personal Data Protection Act requires businesses to disclose the period, area, recipients and manner of use, and Article 3 protects the right to inquire and request deletion.

1.Why check at all

Children's learning apps often need a child's name, voice or usage records. The US Federal Trade Commission explains that the COPPA Rule is designed to put parents in control of what information is collected from their young children online, and that it applies to websites and apps (including mobile apps) directed to children under 13 that collect, use or disclose their personal information. (FTC: COPPA FAQ) The FTC page notes the Rule was amended on 22 April 2025, so check the amended Rule for the exact requirements.

2.Five checks before downloading

CheckWhat to look forBasis
1. What data is collectedDoes the privacy policy list specific items (name, voice, usage records, device information), not vague wording such as "necessary data"?PDPA Art. 8: collectors must disclose purpose and categories of data
2. Is parental consent neededFor services directed to under-13s, is there a parental-consent step, rather than the child simply tapping "agree"?COPPA: notice to parents and verifiable parental consent
3. Who receives the dataDoes it mention cloud, voice or analytics providers? Is data processed outside Taiwan?PDPA Art. 8: disclose the period, area, recipients and manner of use
4. How long it is keptIs there a concrete retention period rather than "indefinitely"?COPPA: keep only as long as necessary and delete with reasonable measures
5. How to request deletionIs there a clear contact channel and response time?PDPA Art. 3: rights to inquire, review, correct, stop use and request deletion, which cannot be waived in advance

The text of Taiwan's PDPA is at the Laws and Regulations Database (Chinese).

3.What parents can do

1. Read the store disclosures first. If a privacy policy link is missing or vague, do not download yet.

2. Do not register with a child's real name or photo. Use a nickname where possible, and do not upload photos if the service does not need them.

3. Review and delete regularly. When you stop using it, request deletion under the policy and remove the app.

4. Use it together. Using the app with your child and talking about it gives more control than leaving it to the child.

Frequently asked questions

Q1: What should I check before downloading a language app for my child?
Five things: what data it collects, whether parental consent is needed, who receives the data, how long it is kept, and how to request deletion.
Q2: What is COPPA?
The US Children's Online Privacy Protection Rule. For online services directed to children under 13, it requires notice to parents and verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information, and retention only as long as necessary. It was amended on 22 April 2025.
Q3: How does Taiwan's law protect a child's personal data?
Article 8 of the Personal Data Protection Act requires disclosure of purpose and the period, area, recipients and manner of use; Article 3 protects the rights to inquire, review, correct, stop use and request deletion, which cannot be waived in advance.
