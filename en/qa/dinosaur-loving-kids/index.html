My Child Loves Dinosaurs: From a Picture Book to a Museum

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-31   ·  中文

The short answer

You can connect "book, museum, draw it at home" into a path: first learn about a few dinosaur species from books, then visit a museum to find the real specimens and fossils, then have the child draw what they saw when they get home. The National Museum of Natural Science in Taichung's Life Science Hall has a "Dinosaur Age" exhibit with a mechanical T. rex 7 meters long and 4 meters tall that moves and roars, as well as a Tarbosaurus skull, a juvenile T. rex skeleton, and more; the National Taiwan Museum in Taipei's Paleontology Gallery has the permanent exhibition "Great Paleontology Exhibition — The Epic of Life and the Dance of Evolution," featuring Huanghetitan, Tarbosaurus, Velociraptor, and other species.

2. Before, during, and after the visit

Here are some practical suggestions:

Before: pick 3 "dinosaurs we're looking for today." Choose the child's 3 favorites from a book; having a mission at the museum makes the visit more purposeful.

During: let the child be the tour guide. Ask "what do you think it ate?" "Does it look the same as the one in the book?" Answers don't need to be correct; the goal is to get the child talking. For questioning techniques, see How to do dialogic reading.

After: draw it, then tell someone about it. Have the child draw the dinosaur they saw on paper, then tell a family member about it.

3. Things you can discuss with the child while there

The NTM exhibition notes state that Earth has experienced at least five mass extinction events; and in Taiwan, the Penghu Channel and the Zuozhen area of Tainan have preserved large quantities of Pleistocene terrestrial vertebrate fossils, known as the "Penghu Fauna" and the "Zuozhen Fauna" respectively. (NTM Great Paleontology Exhibition introduction) These are not dinosaur fossils, but they can help children understand that the land of Taiwan has also buried animals from long ago.

FAQ

Q1: Where in Taiwan can I take my child to see dinosaurs?
The National Museum of Natural Science Life Science Hall in Taichung has a "Dinosaur Age" exhibit, and the National Taiwan Museum Paleontology Gallery in Taipei has the permanent exhibition "Great Paleontology Exhibition — The Epic of Life and the Dance of Evolution." Check each museum's official website for opening hours and admission prices.
Q2: Where is the mechanical T. rex at the NMNS?
It is on the 1st floor of the National Museum of Natural Science Life Science Hall, in the "Dinosaur Age" exhibit. According to the museum's description, the mechanical T. rex is 7 meters long and 4 meters tall and moves and roars, with a pair of mechanical Velociraptors nearby.
Q3: Have fossils been found in Taiwan?
Yes. According to the NTM exhibition notes, the Penghu Channel and the Zuozhen area of Tainan have preserved large quantities of Pleistocene terrestrial vertebrate fossils, known as the "Penghu Fauna" and the "Zuozhen Fauna"; these are not dinosaur fossils.
