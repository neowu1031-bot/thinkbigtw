Sharing Your Child's Photo for a Custom Picture Book: 6 Privacy Questions to Ask First

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-12   ·  中文

The short answer

Before submitting a child's photo to a custom picture book service, clarify 6 things: what the photos will be used for, whether they will be used to train AI, which companies will process them (and whether they will be sent abroad), how they will be stored, when they will be deleted (and what parts cannot be deleted), and how you can request to view or delete them. Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act Article 8 requires providers to proactively notify you of these matters when collecting personal data; Article 3 guarantees your rights to inquire, stop use, and request deletion, and these rights cannot be waived in advance by contract.

1. Why a child's photo needs special care

A front-facing photo of a child can directly identify who that person is. The Personal Data Protection Act Article 2 lists "characteristics" and "other data that can directly or indirectly identify an individual" as personal data (Laws and Regulations Database), so photos used in a custom picture book are regulated by the PDPA from the moment they are uploaded.

Another reason is that the child cannot decide for themselves yet. The parents are making this book, but the person in the photo is the child; when choosing a service, thinking "if this photo were to be leaked, would my child mind when they grow up?" helps avoid overlooking important questions.

The Basic AI Act, promulgated and implemented on 14 January 2026, Article 4 also lists "privacy protection and data governance" as one of the principles of AI development, requiring that personal data be properly protected and that data minimization (collecting and using only necessary data) be applied (Laws and Regulations Database). This can serve as a reference standard for choosing an AI custom service.

A reputable provider should have answers to all 6 questions in their privacy policy. If answers are missing or vaguely written, ask for clarification before ordering.

3. 3 things parents can do themselves

1. Only submit the necessary photos. Include only your own child in the photos, not other children; submit only as many photos as the provider requests.

2. Send photos through the provider's designated official channel. Do not post the child's photos in public comments or on social media posts.

3. After receiving the book, remember the deletion. Check it against the deletion timeline the provider stated; to request early deletion, you can submit a request directly under PDPA Article 3.

FAQ

Q1: Is it safe to use a child's photo for a custom picture book?
Whether it is safe depends on how the provider handles the photos. Before ordering, confirm 6 things: the purpose of the photos, whether they will be used to train AI, which companies will process them, how they will be stored, when they will be deleted, and how to request viewing or deletion. These are all matters that PDPA Article 8 requires providers to notify.
Q2: If the provider says photos will be deleted, does that mean everything is deleted?
Not necessarily. There are three common exceptions: photos in messaging app or shopping platform chat rooms (retained by the platform), data the AI service provider retains under its own policy, and invoices and order records legally required to be kept. A proper privacy policy will state these exceptions clearly.
Q3: Can I request the provider to delete my child's photos?
Yes. PDPA Article 3 guarantees the data subject's rights to inquire, view, obtain copies, correct, stop collection and processing, and request deletion; these rights cannot be waived in advance or limited by contract.
Q4: Will Tale With You use a child's photos to train AI?
No. Under Tale With You's Privacy Policy v1.3, photos are used only to produce this one book and are not used to train any AI model; the OpenAI account used for production has "improve the model with data" disabled. All files for the order are deleted within 3 days of the book's delivery.
