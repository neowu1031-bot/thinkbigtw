Can Parents Be Drawn Into a Custom Picture Book Too? What to Know When Adults Are in the Photo

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-27   ·  中文

The short answer

Yes, but an adult's photo is also personal data, so the person in the photo must give their own consent first. Using Tale With You as an example, you can optionally submit photos of 1–2 adults, 1–2 solo photos per adult, at no extra charge; the main character is still the child. Any adult appearing in photos must be an adult who gives their own consent. If the second adult (such as the other parent or a grandparent) is someone other than the person placing the order, their consent must be obtained first. Before ordering you can also ask the provider how they obtain consent from other adults in the photos — for example, whether consent must be given directly by that person.

1. Why an adult's photo also requires that person's consent

The Personal Data Protection Act Article 2 lists "characteristics" and "other data that can directly or indirectly identify an individual" as personal data (Laws and Regulations Database). A front-facing photo of a father or mother, just like a child's photo, can directly identify who that person is.

For a provider (a "non-public entity" in legal terms) to collect personal data, Article 19 requires a specific purpose and compliance with one of the statutory conditions, such as having a contractual relationship with the data subject or obtaining the data subject's consent. The adult in the photo is not necessarily the person placing the order and may not have a contract with the provider, so the question is whether that adult has given their own consent.

Therefore, before placing an order, confirm how the provider obtains consent from other adults in the photos — for example, whether that adult must express consent directly. Additionally, Article 8 lists what a provider must notify when collecting personal data: the name of the collecting entity, the purpose of collection, the category of data, the period, territory, recipients, and methods of use, and the rights the data subject may exercise; this can be checked against the provider's privacy policy.

Adults themselves also have rights under Article 3: to inquire, view, request deletion, request cessation of collection, processing, or use, and so on; these rights cannot be waived in advance by contract.

2. 5 things to confirm before ordering

  1. Who will appear? First count clearly which adults will appear, then check how many the provider can illustrate at one time.
  2. Has every person consented? How does the provider confirm? It is simplest when only the person placing the order appears; for another parent or elder to appear, first explain the photo's purpose and the deletion process to them and get their agreement before submitting. Also ask the provider how they obtain consent from other adults in the photos — for example, whether consent must be given directly by that person.
  3. Does each photo show only that adult? Do not use a group photo, to avoid submitting someone who hasn't consented.
  4. Which pages will the adult appear on? In a custom picture book the main character is usually the child; ask in advance which pages the adult will appear on based on the story, to avoid unmet expectations.
  5. What if the adult changes their mind? Ask the provider whether the adult in the photo can apply to have their data deleted themselves, and how that is handled before and after printing.

For questions about a child's photos, see How is privacy protected when using a child's photo for a custom picture book? This page covers only adults' photos.

How Tale With You handles adults appearing in the book

Tale With You's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service (v1.3, updated 10 October 2026) state the following about adults appearing in the book:

  1. May appear, same price: Optional. 1–2 adults, 1–2 solo front-facing photos per adult, with only that adult in the photo. The adult will appear as an AI illustration in the book; which pages they appear on depends on the story; the main character is still the child.
  2. Personal consent required: Any adult appearing in photos must be an adult who gives their own consent. When a second adult (such as the other parent or a grandparent) is involved, the person placing the order must first obtain that adult's consent and show them the explanation in the "children's photo consent form." The person placing the order can also ask the provider how they obtain consent from other adults in the photos — for example, whether consent must be given directly by that person.
  3. Only illustrates faces of adults who have submitted photos: In scenes that require an adult's presence (or when no adult photo has been submitted), the adult is drawn from behind, in profile, or from the shoulders down — no unfamiliar adult's face will be drawn.
  4. Same handling as the child's photos: Used only for this one book, not for advertisements. The OpenAI account used for production has had "improve the model with data" disabled; according to OpenAI's documentation, after disabling this setting, new conversations will not be used to train their model. All files for this order are deleted within 3 days of the book's delivery. Data OpenAI retains under its own policy, as well as messages on the Shopee or LINE platforms, are outside the deletion scope; see the Privacy Policy for details.
  5. The adult can apply for deletion directly: The adult in the photo can contact the provider directly via the official LINE or Email to request deletion or withdrawal of consent. If the request is made before printing, their photo will be deleted and their face will no longer be illustrated; the person who placed the order may also choose to cancel the order for a full refund. If the book has already been sent for printing or shipped, the physical book cannot be recalled.

The illustration is drawn by AI based on the photo and may not exactly match the person's appearance; a preview will be sent for confirmation before printing. If the adult's illustration is not a close likeness, the free regeneration included with each order can be used, or the option to not illustrate the adult's face can be chosen.

FAQ

Q1: Can parents both appear together in a custom picture book?
Yes, but it depends on the provider's rules. Using Tale With You as an example, you can optionally submit 1–2 adults with 1–2 solo photos per adult at no extra charge; the main character is still the child; any adult in the photos must be an adult who gives their own consent.
Q2: If you upload a photo of your partner or an elder, is their consent needed?
Yes. A front-facing adult photo is also personal data. Using Tale With You as an example, when a second adult is involved, the person placing the order must first obtain that adult's consent; you can also ask the provider how consent from other adults in the photos is obtained — for example, whether it must be given directly by that person.
Q3: Can the adult in the photo later request deletion?
Yes. Personal Data Protection Act Article 3 guarantees the data subject's right to request deletion and cessation of collection, processing, or use. Using Tale With You as an example, the adult in the photo can apply directly; before printing, their photo will be deleted and their face will no longer be illustrated; once the book has been sent for printing or shipped, the physical book cannot be recalled.
Q4: If no adult photo is submitted, will an adult appear in the book?
Using Tale With You as an example, in scenes requiring an adult's presence, the adult is drawn from behind, in profile, or from the shoulders down — no unfamiliar adult's face will be drawn; an adult's face in the book is illustrated only from the submitted photo.
