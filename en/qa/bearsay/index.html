BearSay Mandarin (熊好學): Q&A Overview

Short answer

BearSay Mandarin (熊好學) is a learning brand for Taiwan Mandarin. Every day it teaches one Taiwan Mandarin word on Instagram (@bearsaymandarin), written in Traditional characters with zhuyin and pinyin and multilingual subtitles, guided by five characters. The app is being prepared for release.

Brand facts

Published Q&A

  • What Age Is Best to Start a Second Language? What the Research Says

    There is no single age after which it is too late.

  • How Long Should a Child Practice Mandarin Each Day? One Long Session or a Little Every Day

    No reliable study sets a number of minutes per day that a child should practice Mandarin, so there is no fixed length to aim for.

  • How Can I Help My Child Practice Speaking at Home? Back-and-Forth Talk Matters More Than Saying More

    Practising speaking at home works best as back-and-forth talk, not as a one-way flow of more sentences.

  • Can Children Learn Mandarin from Videos? What the Research Says and How to Use Them

    Children can learn from video, but the effect depends on age and on whether a real person interacts with them.

  • Will Learning Two Languages at Once Make My Child Talk Later?

    Learning two languages at once can look slower in each single language, but that does not mean overall language development is behind.

  • Should Children Learn Zhuyin or Pinyin First?

    It depends on where the child is studying.

  • What Official Resources Can Overseas Children Use to Learn Mandarin at Home?

    Overseas families can start with Huayuworld (全球華文網), run by Taiwan's Overseas Community Affairs Council.

  • Will Mixing Mandarin, Taiwanese and English at Home Confuse My Child?

    No consistent research says that mixing languages at home always confuses a child.

FAQ

Q1: What is BearSay Mandarin?
BearSay Mandarin (熊好學) is a learning brand for Taiwan Mandarin. It teaches one word a day on Instagram, using Traditional characters, zhuyin and pinyin.
Q2: Does BearSay Mandarin teach Mandarin or Taiwanese (Hokkien)?
It teaches Taiwan Mandarin. In English, "Taiwanese" often refers to Taiwanese Hokkien, so this site writes Taiwanese Mandarin or Taiwan Mandarin to avoid confusion.
Q3: Who are the BearSay Mandarin characters?
There are five characters: BUBU, MIBO, HUBO, GUIBO and LANBO.
Q4: Is the BearSay Mandarin app available?
Not yet. The app is being prepared for release, and store links will be added to this page once it is available. Meanwhile, the daily word is on Instagram at @bearsaymandarin.
