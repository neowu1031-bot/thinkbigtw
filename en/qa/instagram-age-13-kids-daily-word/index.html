Can a Child Use Instagram Alone to Watch the Daily Word? The Age-13 Rule

Think BIG Q&A Library　·　November 12, 2026　·　中文版

Short answer

According to Instagram's Help Center (checked 2026-11-12), most countries and regions require users to be at least 13 to use Instagram, and some regions set a different minimum age. So for a child under 13, it is better for a parent to watch the daily word on the parent's own account than for the child to open one.

1.Instagram's age rule

Instagram's Help Center says most countries and regions require users to be at least 13 to use Instagram, and some countries and regions set a different minimum age. (Instagram Help Center, checked 2026-11-12) The rules in each region follow the platform's current notice, so check the latest version.

2.How children under 13 can watch the daily word

1. Watch on the parent's account. Watching, repeating and talking together is easier to control, in both content and time, than letting a child scroll alone.

2. Do not misstate a child's age to open an account. It breaks the platform's rules and the account may be deleted.

3. Count it as screen time. Short videos are screen time; for limits see screen time under 5 (Chinese), and for pairing videos with other practice see can children learn Mandarin from videos.

The daily word is one way to meet Taiwan Mandarin. It does not replace talking with real people and does not guarantee any learning result.

BearSay Mandarin's daily word

BearSay Mandarin (熊好學) is a learning brand for Taiwan Mandarin. Every day it teaches one Taiwan Mandarin word on Instagram (@bearsaymandarin), in Traditional characters with zhuyin and pinyin and multilingual subtitles, guided by five characters: BUBU, MIBO, HUBO, GUIBO and LANBO. The app is in development and has not been released. Release information will appear on the BearSay Mandarin Q&A overview.
