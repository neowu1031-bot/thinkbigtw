Are 'Guaranteed to Learn' and 'Results in Weeks' Claims Credible? How Parents Can Read Learning App Ads

Think BIG Q&A Library　·　November 12, 2026　·　中文版

Short answer

Learning results depend on each child, the amount of exposure and how the app is used, so no app can guarantee a result for every child. Article 22 of Taiwan's Consumer Protection Act requires businesses to ensure that advertising is truthful and their obligations may not be lower than the advertising; Article 21 of the Fair Trade Act prohibits false or misleading representations on matters affecting a purchase decision. When you see 'guaranteed to learn' or 'results in weeks', check the basis, the target child, the conditions and the refund terms.

1.How Taiwan's rules treat advertising

LawKey point
Consumer Protection Act, Article 22Businesses must ensure the truthfulness of advertising; their obligations to consumers may not be lower than the advertising; after a contract is formed, advertised content must be fulfilled. Laws and Regulations Database (Chinese)
Fair Trade Act, Article 21Businesses may not make false or misleading representations, on goods or in advertising, about matters relevant to goods that affect a transaction decision (including quality, content and use); it applies to services too. Laws and Regulations Database (Chinese)

2.Four things parents can check

1. What is the basis? Does "results in weeks" state how it was measured and for whom, or is it only a slogan?

2. Who is it for? Which age and level does the claim apply to? Individual differences are large, and one claim rarely fits every child.

3. Where are the conditions? How many minutes a day and combined with what? Promises without stated conditions deserve a discount.

4. What do the refund and cancellation terms say? Read them before paying; for the legal points see child in-app purchases and refunds.

Research conclusions on language development are mostly "correlational" or "under specific conditions", for example the research on AI voice versus live conversation; none can be read as a result guarantee for any product. Frequently asked questions

Q1: Is a 'guaranteed to learn' claim credible?
Results depend on each child, the amount of exposure and how the app is used, so no app can guarantee a result for every child. Check the basis, target, conditions and refund terms.
Q2: What does the law say about truthful advertising?
Article 22 of the Consumer Protection Act requires businesses to ensure advertising is truthful, with obligations not lower than the advertising; Article 21 of the Fair Trade Act prohibits false or misleading representations.
Q3: What should I check when I see 'results in weeks'?
The basis (how it was measured), the target (which kind of child), the conditions (how long each day) and the refund and cancellation terms.
Q4: Does BearSay Mandarin guarantee learning results?
BearSay Mandarin does not guarantee any learning result and does not replace talking with real people.
