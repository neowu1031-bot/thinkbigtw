What Official Resources Can Overseas Children Use to Learn Mandarin at Home?

Think BIG Q&A Library　·　November 9, 2026　·　中文版

Short answer

Overseas families can start with Huayuworld (全球華文網), run by Taiwan's Overseas Community Affairs Council. Its series Let's Learn Mandarin (For Children) is built on second-language teaching concepts, with 12 units per volume containing dialogues, vocabulary lists, sentence-pattern practice, rhymes and games. Phonetic marks are zhuyin and pinyin, with Simplified characters noted alongside, and it suits primary to junior-high ages. The site also has a games section and a Traditional-character resource section. Whether content is free or needs registration follows the site's own notices.

2.Using them at home

Short and regular. Rather than finishing a volume in one sitting, take a small slice each day (see daily practice time).

People still need to speak Mandarin. A child's performance in each language is related to how much input there is in that language (see two languages and speech delay). Materials give content, but speaking chances come from family, friends and community.

Start with one phonetic system. The series marks both zhuyin and pinyin, so the child can begin with the one that feels familiar (see zhuyin or pinyin first).

Look up sounds in the official dictionary. The Ministry of Education's Revised Mandarin Dictionary gives pronunciations and meanings: dict.revised.moe.edu.tw.

3.Things to note

The series notes Simplified characters. If a family wants the child to meet mainly Traditional characters, the Traditional-character section can be used alongside. Try a sample first to see whether it suits your child; this site does not rank teaching materials.

Brand information | BearSay Mandarin

BearSay Mandarin for overseas families

BearSay Mandarin (熊好學) is a learning brand for Taiwan Mandarin. Every day it teaches one Taiwan Mandarin word on Instagram (@bearsaymandarin), in Traditional characters with zhuyin and pinyin and multilingual subtitles, guided by five characters: BUBU, MIBO, HUBO, GUIBO and LANBO. The app is in development and has not been released. Release information will appear on the BearSay Mandarin Q&A overview.

BearSay Mandarin's daily word uses Traditional characters, zhuyin and pinyin with multilingual subtitles, and can be found on Instagram by families overseas. It can be used alongside official materials but does not replace them, and does not guarantee any learning result.
