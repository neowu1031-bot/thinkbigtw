Can I Return a Custom Picture Book? The 7-Day Cooling-Off Period and Its Exceptions

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-17   ·  中文

The short answer

Under Taiwan's Consumer Protection Act Article 19, most goods bought online can be returned within 7 days without giving a reason. However, the Regulations on Reasonable Exceptions to Right to Cancel Distance Transactions, Article 2(2), allows sellers to exclude 'customized goods made per the consumer's request' from this right—provided the seller clearly notifies the consumer before the order is placed. If the notification happens and the buyer confirms, the customized book is not returnable after production begins. The right to a remedy for defective goods (broken binding, missing pages, blurry print) remains under the Civil Code.

1. Taiwan's 7-day cooling-off rule and the customized-goods exception

Taiwan Consumer Protection Act, Article 19, states that for home-visit sales or mail-order (distance sales) transactions, consumers may rescind the contract within 7 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason, and the seller may not stipulate otherwise. This is a mandatory consumer right.

However, the same law authorizes the Executive Yuan to define categories of reasonable exceptions. The Regulations on Reasonable Exceptions to Right to Cancel Distance Transactions (last updated September 23, 2015) list these at Article 2, item 2: "Goods made or processed according to the consumer's personal request or specifications."

Two conditions must both be met for a seller to invoke this exception:

  1. The goods genuinely are customized to the specific consumer's specifications.
  2. The seller notifies the consumer of this exception before the transaction takes place, and the consumer confirms awareness.

If condition 2 is not met, the exception does not apply even if the goods are customized. Consumer complaints: 1950 (Executive Yuan Consumer Protection Officer hotline).

2. Defective goods: separate rights remain

Even when the 7-day return right is validly excluded, the Civil Code seller's warranty against defects still applies. Under Civil Code Articles 354 and 359, if the delivered goods have defects that make them unfit for ordinary use or that substantially reduce their value, the buyer may rescind the contract or request a price reduction. What counts as a defect in a custom picture book might include: broken binding, missing pages, blurry printing, colors clearly differing from the confirmed preview, or the wrong child's photos in the pages.

3. Three moments when you can still stop or change the order

Before submitting photos / before the preview: At this stage no production has started. You can cancel for any reason, and a full refund applies.

After the preview, before confirming: You can request text changes (unlimited) and ask for one full regeneration free of charge. Only after you actively confirm the preview does production begin.

After confirming: Production starts; content changes are no longer possible. If a print defect exists upon delivery, the remedy is a free reprint, not a refund or exchange.

FAQ

Q1: Can I return a custom picture book under Taiwan's 7-day return rule?
Custom goods made to a consumer's specification are an exception under the Regulations on Reasonable Exceptions to Right to Cancel Distance Transactions, Article 2(2)—but only if the seller notified you of this exception before the order was placed. If proper notice was given, the 7-day return right does not apply after production starts.
Q2: When can I still cancel a custom picture book order?
Any time before printing begins (before you confirm the preview). After confirmation, production starts and content changes are no longer possible.
Q3: What if the book is defective?
Defects are handled separately from the 7-day return right under Civil Code seller warranty provisions. Tale With You provides a free reprint for qualifying defects (broken binding, missing pages, blurry printing, colors clearly different from the confirmed preview) reported within 7 days of delivery with photos.
Q4: What is the difference between cancelling and reporting a defect?
Cancellation (before printing): full refund. Defect remedy (after delivery): free reprint. The 7-day cooling-off return for change of mind does not apply to validly notified customized goods. The defect remedy exists independently and is not affected by the customized-goods exception.
