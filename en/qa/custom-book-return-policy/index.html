Shared Reading & Custom Picture Books Q07

Can I Return a Custom Picture Book? The 7-Day Cooling-Off Period and Its Exceptions

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-17   ·  中文

The short answer

Most custom picture books do not qualify for the seven-day inspection period. Consumer Protection Act Article 19 gives online shoppers the right to cancel within 7 days of receiving a product without giving a reason, but the Executive Yuan's Regulations Governing Reasonable Exceptions to the Right of Rescission in Distance Transactions Article 2 lists "customized products made at the consumer's request" as an exception, provided the business notifies the consumer in advance. Not qualifying for the seven-day inspection period does not mean there is no recourse if a defective product is received: if the book has defects, rescission or a price reduction can still be requested under the Civil Code.

1. What is the seven-day inspection period

Consumer Protection Act Article 19 provides that consumers in distance transactions (online shopping, TV shopping, etc.) may rescind the contract within seven days of receiving the product by returning the product or giving written notice, without stating a reason and without bearing any cost. However, the same article also includes a proviso: "distance transactions with reasonable exceptional circumstances are not subject to this," with the exceptions set by the Executive Yuan. (Consumer Protection Act)

The same article contains another protection that is often overlooked: if the business fails to provide the information related to rescission as required, the seven-day period starts from the day after the information is provided, up to a maximum of four months.

2. Why custom products are an exception

The Regulations Governing Reasonable Exceptions to the Right of Rescission in Distance Transactions Article 2 lists 7 types of exceptions, the second of which is "customized products made at the consumer's request." (Regulations Governing Reasonable Exceptions) A book made according to your child's photos and name cannot be sold to anyone else after it is made, so it falls into this category.

However, the regulations explicitly state the prerequisite: these exceptions only exclude the seven-day inspection period if the consumer has been notified by the business. So before placing the order, the business should clearly tell you "this is a custom product and does not qualify for the seven-day inspection period."

3. Even without no-reason returns, there are still these protections

Defects are handled separately. Civil Code Article 354 requires the seller to guarantee that the product is free from defects that reduce its value or utility; Article 359 provides that in the event of defects, the buyer may rescind the contract or request a price reduction. (Civil Code) Missing pages, printing errors, and binding failures are separate from the "seven-day inspection period."

Advertisements count. Consumer Protection Act Article 22 requires businesses to ensure the accuracy of advertising content, and their obligations to consumers may not fall below the advertising content. The dimensions, page count, and content described on the product page are things you can demand.

Disputes can be reported. First contact the business's customer service; if that fails, call the National Consumer Service Hotline at 1950, or file a complaint with the consumer service center of your local county or city government. (Executive Yuan Consumer Protection Commission)

4. 4 things to check before ordering

1. Is it clearly stated that the seven-day inspection period does not apply? Written in a place visible before checkout, not buried in long terms.

2. Can the order be cancelled before printing and what is the refund process? Custom products are usually "once printed, cannot be cancelled"—so what about before printing?

3. Is there a preview before printing? How many revisions are allowed? Seeing the preview before printing is the most practical protection for custom products.

4. How are defects handled? What counts as a defect, whether reprinting requires shipping fees, and within what period you must contact them.

FAQ

Q1: Do custom products qualify for the seven-day inspection period?
Usually not. Consumer Protection Act Article 19 gives online shoppers a 7-day no-reason right of rescission, but the Regulations Governing Reasonable Exceptions Article 2(2) lists "customized products made at the consumer's request" as an exception, provided the business notifies the consumer in advance.
Q2: If the business doesn't state that the seven-day inspection period doesn't apply, can I still return it?
The regulations state that the exception only excludes the seven-day inspection period if the consumer is notified by the business. If the business did not notify in advance, it cannot claim this exception.
Q3: What do I do if the custom picture book I received has defects?
Defects and the seven-day inspection period are separate matters. Under Civil Code Articles 354 and 359, if a product has defects, the buyer may rescind the contract or request a price reduction; you may also first request a reprint under the business's defect handling policy.
Q4: Can a Tale With You order be cancelled?
An order can be cancelled before printing. Under Tale With You's Terms of Service v1.3, cancellation and full refund are available at any time before the book is sent to print; already-printed custom products cannot be cancelled, but printing defects are eligible for a free reprint.
