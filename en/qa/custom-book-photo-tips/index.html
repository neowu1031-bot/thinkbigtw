What Photos Should I Take for a Custom Picture Book? 5 Tips for a Clear Face

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-28   ·  中文

The short answer

The key to a photo that produces a clear AI illustration is "face forward, well-lit, only this child." The Ministry of Foreign Affairs' passport photo guidelines (2025-08-29) specify: neutral expression facing the camera, full face visible, eyes open and looking at the lens, uniform light background, good lighting, no shadows on the face. These are also good benchmarks for custom picture book photos.

1. Five tips for taking the photo

TipHome practicePassport photo benchmark
1. Face forwardAsk the child to look at the camera; call their name or use a small toy to get their attention.Full face visible, facing forward, eyes looking at the lens.
2. Good lightingUse natural light from a window, stand facing the window; avoid backlighting or strong side shadows.Even lighting, no shadows on the face.
3. Only the childMove other people and large objects out of frame; keep the background simple.Only the applicant; a simple, uniform, light-colored background.
4. Eyes openWait for the child to look up naturally; for young children, try after waking from a nap.Eyes open and clearly visible.
5. Clear, not blurryKeep the camera steady; for a smartphone, use the rear camera (usually higher resolution).Photo must be clear, without distortion or blurriness.

Passport photo standards come from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) 2025-08-29 guidelines; the right column's home practice descriptions are written based on these standards and are not official text. (MOFA Passport Photo Specifications)

2. How many photos to provide

Providing 1–3 photos is usually sufficient. Multiple photos of the same face from different angles help the AI capture features more accurately. If the provider's instructions say a specific number, follow those instructions.

3. What if the existing photos aren't ideal?

If you don't have a suitable photo, taking a new one is an option. Find good natural light, let the child sit in a chair, hold a small toy to attract their gaze, and take several shots. A recent photo (taken within the past six months) is recommended, as the child's appearance changes quickly.

FAQ

Q1: What kind of photo works best for a custom picture book?
"Face forward, well-lit, only this child." The key points are: neutral expression facing the camera, full face visible, eyes open and looking at the lens, good even lighting without facial shadows, and simple background.
Q2: How many photos should I provide?
Providing 1–3 photos is usually sufficient. Multiple photos of the same face from different angles help the AI capture features more accurately. If the provider specifies a number, follow those instructions.
Q3: Can I use a group photo?
It is not recommended. The photo should contain only the child to avoid including people who have not consented; a background with fewer distractions also makes it easier for the AI to focus on the child's facial features.
