What Photos Should I Take for a Custom Picture Book? 5 Tips for a Clear Face

Think BIG Q&A Library  ·  2026-10-28   ·  中文

The short answer

The most important quality in a photo for a custom picture book is a clear, unobstructed view of the face, taken straight-on with good lighting. AI illustration tools work from facial features, so a blurry, side-on, or back-lit photo produces a less accurate likeness. Taiwan's Bureau of Consular Affairs (BOCA) passport photo specifications, based on ICAO standards, provide a useful reference—even though they apply to identity documents, the principles (front-facing, uniform lighting, no shadows, no beauty filters) produce the kind of photo that AI face-capture tools work best with.

1. Five tips adapted from BOCA standards

Taiwan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs Bureau of Consular Affairs updated its passport photo specifications on August 29, 2025, based on ICAO standards. While you are not making a passport photo, the principles transfer directly to a custom book photo:

1. Face forward, eyes open, neutral expression. The face should be centered in the frame, eyes open and looking at the camera. A slight smile is fine; a big laugh or looking away cuts off facial features the AI needs. The ears do not need to show, but the full face from hairline to chin should be visible.

2. Uniform lighting with no shadows. Natural light near a window is ideal—soft, even, and free. Avoid direct sunlight creating half-shadow across the face; avoid a lamp positioned to one side. BOCA specifically prohibits shadows across the face, red-eye from flash, and glare from eyeglasses.

3. Use the rear camera, not the front (selfie) camera. BOCA notes that the front camera may cause mirror-image reversal or wide-angle distortion; both reduce face-capture accuracy. For a child photo, ask another adult to take the shot with the rear camera while the child faces them.

4. No beauty filters or face retouching. AI face-capture tools learn from actual facial features. Filters that smooth skin, change eye size, or reshape the face produce output that does not look like the actual child. Disable beauty/AI filters before shooting.

5. Plain background, only the child (or the child with the consenting adult) in frame. BOCA specifies no other people's hands, objects, or chair backs. For infants: no high-chair back, no pacifiers, no hands of others. For older children: no other children in the same frame.

2. Before sending the photo: privacy checklist

  • Strip GPS metadata. Smartphone photos can contain exact location coordinates. Before sending, use your phone's built-in "share without location" option or a metadata-stripping app.
  • Avoid school uniforms or school names in the frame. These reveal which school the child attends—information that should not be shared alongside a photo to a third party.
  • Avoid house numbers, street signs, or identifiable home locations in the background.
  • Send via the official channel only. Do not post the photo publicly on social media or in comment sections.

3. What to do if the first attempt is not good

If the AI illustration in the preview does not capture the child's likeness well, Tale With You offers one free full regeneration per order (whole book, not page by page). To make the most of this, send a different photo with the regeneration request—ideally one taken with better lighting or a more neutral expression—so the second attempt has better input data.

FAQ

Q1: What is the most important thing in a photo for a custom picture book?
A clear, front-facing view of the face with good even lighting and no beauty filters. AI face-capture tools work from actual facial features; blurry, side-on, or heavily filtered photos produce less accurate illustrations.
Q2: Should I use the front or rear camera?
Rear camera. Taiwan's BOCA passport photo specifications note that the front (selfie) camera may cause mirror-image reversal or wide-angle distortion—both reduce face-capture accuracy.
Q3: How many photos should I send for a custom book?
Tale With You accepts 1–3 frontal photos. Sending 2–3 photos from slightly different angles gives the AI more information to work from, which often improves likeness consistency across the 26 pages.
Q4: What if I don't like how the AI drew my child?
One free full regeneration is included per order. For best results, send a different photo with the regeneration request—different lighting or a more neutral expression—so the second attempt has better input.
