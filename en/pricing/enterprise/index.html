Enterprise use

Enterprise Deployment: On-Premises and Cloud-Assisted

Enterprise deployment architecture is chosen based on your data handling policy, team collaboration needs, and maintenance responsibilities. Both cloud-assisted and on-premises options use a requirements-first, contract-based approach — data flow, access boundaries, acceptance testing, and SLA are all confirmed upfront.

Business plan: from NT$60,000

How it differs from the personal annual plan: used by multiple people in a company, a knowledge base built from company data, local-model deployment and a security review. Hardware not included.

The business plan includes an AI readiness check: with your company's written consent, we take stock of software usage time and file types on the computers, without reading contents or recording passwords, to find the first thing AI is best placed to do.

Initial deployment assessment

1. Does your data policy allow cloud processing of company data?
2. What is your primary deployment consideration?
3. Is this requirement for personal use only?

Architecture recommendations are based on your answers. Formal configuration requires a separate requirements assessment.

Enterprise deployment options

Enterprise 1 · Cloud

Cloud-Assisted

All three tiers (Starter / Standard / Full) are available with either deployment option.

Integrates cloud model APIs using your corporate accounts and authorisation policies, establishing a managed AI Agent environment for each department. Data fields transmitted, service terms, and access roles are confirmed before deployment — bringing distributed AI use under a managed framework.

  • Dedicated custom Agent per department; departments are isolated, usage monitored independently
  • Employee allowlist channels (Slack / Teams / LINE Business Account etc.)
  • Deployment process: requirements interview → quote → contract → deployment
  • Support period and SLA: per contract

Enterprise 2 · On-Premises

On-Premises

Deploys local AI models and AI Agents on company-designated hardware, with access scope configured according to your data policy. External integrations, logs, and backups are confirmed separately — maintaining full data sovereignty and operational boundaries.

  • Local deployment: model and data stay on company machines
  • Pre-change backups with rollback; ongoing maintenance and technical support per contract
  • Deployment process: requirements interview → quote → contract → deployment
  • Support period and SLA: per contract
