5 things that change the price

Factor 01

One person or the whole company

Personal plans run on your own computer at a fixed price. When several people use it, we have to set up accounts and permissions, split work by department and turn company documents into a knowledge base. That is why the Business Plan starts at NT$52,600 and is quoted to scope.

Factor 02

Local model or cloud model

A local model runs on your own device with no usage fee, and your data stays on your computer. A cloud model needs less hardware, but conversations are sent to the model provider for processing and usage is billed separately; we tell you which provider and its data terms before you sign. Personal plans work with either, at the same price.

Factor 03

One-time setup or a full year of support

Single and Dual AI Agent plans are paid once and include 7 days of technical support after installation, with no monthly fee. Annual plans add a year of maintenance and fault repair within 48 hours, so they cost more. Renewing in the second year is your choice.

Factor 04

How many AI agents, and whether they work together

One agent is enough to answer messages, look things up and remember your preferences. Two agents sharing memory and splitting work in a meeting room is what the Dual AI Agent and Flagship plans cover.

Factor 05

Departments, data volume and systems to connect (business)

A Business Plan quote mostly depends on three things: how many department agents you need, how many company documents go into the knowledge base, and whether you need integration with existing systems or an SLA. A full RAG knowledge base is quoted as a project. Apple silicon Macs are the main platform; other devices can be assessed.

FAQ

  • Q1. What is the cheapest way to get an AI assistant set up?
    The lowest price is NT$7,840 for the Single AI Agent Setup: one AI assistant installed on your own computer (OpenClaw or Hermes, same price), with remote installation, a tutorial video and 7 days of technical support after installation. You pay once, with no monthly fee.
  • Q2. How much does it cost for a company to adopt AI?
    The Business Plan starts at NT$52,600 (excl. tax; NT$55,230 incl. tax), hardware not included, and includes an AI readiness check. You can choose Cloud-Assisted or On-Premises. The final price is quoted after a requirements interview.
  • Q3. Does the price include AI model fees?
    Local models carry no usage fee. If you use a cloud model, you apply for your own API key and pay the model provider for what you use. This is not part of the plan price.
  • Q4. Do I need to buy a new computer?
    Personal plans install on the computer you already have. Apple M-series Macs, Windows or Linux with an NVIDIA graphics card, and AMD Ryzen AI Max machines can all be assessed. If your computer is not powerful enough, we switch to a cloud model on the same plan at the same price. The Business Plan does not include hardware; equipment needs are confirmed during the interview.
  • Q5. Do I keep paying every year?
    Single and Dual AI Agent plans are paid once, with no monthly or annual fee. Annual plans can be renewed in the second year if you choose: Premier NT$35,910, Flagship NT$43,010. Business Plan renewal in the second year is also optional and is never charged automatically.
  • Q6. What is the difference between personal and business plans?
    Personal plans are for one person, installed on their own computer, at a fixed price. The Business Plan is for several users: we build a knowledge base from company documents, set up department roles and permissions, and run a security review. It works through a requirements interview and a contract.
  • Q7. Do I get a refund if the installation fails?
    If the installation fails because of a technical problem on our side, you get a full refund. Cancelling before work starts is also fully refunded; stopping after work has started is refunded on a prorated basis according to the steps already completed. Orders placed on Shopee follow Shopee's refund process.
  • Q8. I'm not sure which plan fits. Where do I start?
    Contact us and tell us what repetitive work you want to hand off. For a company deployment, you can also fill in the Enterprise AI Self-Assessment first.
