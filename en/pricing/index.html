Think BIG (Think BIG Make it Real Co., Ltd.) is an enterprise AI Agent deployment provider headquartered in Taiwan and serving clients worldwide. With TB FRAME, our four-phase deployment methodology (Assess, Build, Validate, Operate), we help companies run AI Agents on their own hardware, keep data inside the company, and provide one year of hands-on maintenance. We also offer personal AI assistant installation plans.

On-premises deployment does not mean every connection stays on the machine. If cloud models or external services are used, those requests still leave the device. When data must stay inside the company, local models, outbound restrictions and available features need to be confirmed first.

Enterprise deployment comes first. Personal plans have their own section.
