Personal AI Agent Plans
Single AI Agent Setup, Dual AI Agent, and Annual

Personal plans are for one person: sole traders, freelancers, side-project founders, or executives who want to try it for themselves first. The AI model and AI Agent are installed on your own computer (Apple M-series Mac, Windows + NVIDIA, or AMD Ryzen AI Max all supported — see hardware requirements), then connected to your LINE Business Account or Telegram. Remote installation by Taiwan-based engineers. If your hardware doesn't meet requirements, you can switch to a cloud model at the same plan price. Choose based on your needs: Single AI Agent Setup includes a full skill pack + 7 days of tech support; Dual AI Agent includes a collaboration meeting room + 7-day tech support; Annual plan includes full-year operations.

Can my computer run a local AI model?

Apple M-series Mac, Windows (or Linux) + NVIDIA dedicated GPU, and AMD Ryzen AI Max unified-memory machines can all be evaluated. Only hardware marked "tested" or "confirmed installation experience" reflects machines we have worked on; others are estimates. Send a screenshot of your hardware specs before proceeding. If your hardware doesn't meet requirements, switch to a cloud model at the same plan price.

AMD Ryzen AI Max series (unified memory)

  • Ryzen AI Max+ 395, 64 GB (Windows): confirmed installation experience. Single AI Agent, chat-only: approx. 32–40 seconds per response; web search: approx. 3–4 minutes per response.
  • Other AMD GPUs, AMD machines under 64 GB, or AMD on Linux: evaluated case by case.

Common requirements

  • 40 GB+ free disk space; 60 GB+ recommended for machines with 64 GB+ RAM (larger models)
  • Stable internet on installation day (model download is approx. 7–20 GB); keep the computer on and prevent sleep mode
  • How to screenshot your specs — Mac: Apple menu → "About This Mac". Windows: Ctrl + Shift + Esc → "Performance" tab; take one screenshot each of CPU, Memory, GPU, and remaining disk space.

Response times above are measured on a single machine. Actual speed varies with question length, number of skills, and open applications. Local vs. cloud will be recommended by the engineer after reviewing your hardware screenshots. With a cloud model, your questions and data are sent to the model provider's servers; usage fees are billed by the provider to your own account.

Client responsibilities

  • Local model computation carries no per-usage fee. If you opt for a cloud model, your own API Key is required (we guide you through the process but do not apply on your behalf). Usage fees are billed directly by the model provider.
  • LINE integration requires a Business Account (Messaging API only). Personal LINE accounts are not supported.
  • Optional: clients with an existing Claude Code or Codex subscription can add Coding Agent integration (subscription is your own; we handle the integration setup only).
  • Supabase free account required (self-register) for plans that include the cross-Agent collaboration meeting room.

Refund policy

Full refund if installation cannot be completed due to a technical issue on our side. Service cancellation is handled in accordance with consumer protection law.

Prices listed are service fees only. Local model computation carries no per-usage fee. Cloud model usage fees are billed by the provider to your own account and are not included in the service fee.
