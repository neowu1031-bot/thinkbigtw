Tool: ChatGPT

Funny photo edits: meme face, shocked face, museum portrait

Upload one photo, paste one prompt: exaggerate the expression and keep the face recognisable.

How to Open the ChatGPT app, tap "+", pick a photo of yourself or your own pet, then paste one prompt. Start a new chat for each new photo.

1 | Meme face

Turn this photo into a funny meme. Keep the subject's original face, hairstyle, clothes and background so they are instantly recognisable as the same person; change only the expression to an exaggerated one: eyes wide open, one eyebrow high and one low, mouth twisted to one side as if holding back a laugh, like a chat-app sticker. Do not change body shape, skin tone or physical features. No text. Vertical 4:5.

2 | Shocked face

Keep the subject of the photo, instantly recognisable as the same person or the same pet, and change the expression to an exaggerated shocked face: eyes as wide as possible, mouth in an O shape, hair or ears sticking up, as if a snack just fell on the floor. Make it funny, not scary, and never injured or sad. Do not change body shape, skin tone, fur colour or physical features. No text. Vertical 4:5.

3 | Museum-portrait parody

Paint this photo as a classical oil portrait, like one hanging on a museum wall: fine brushwork, dark background, gold frame. Keep the subject's face, hairstyle or fur colour so they are instantly recognisable; an overly solemn pose with a deadpan expression that is funny: side-eye, a hint of a suppressed smile. Do not change body shape, skin tone or physical features. No text. Do not imitate any specific famous painting. Vertical 4:5.
  • Only edit photos of yourself or your own pet; ask friends first. The joke is the expression and the style, never anyone's looks, body or skin tone.
  • Label what you post as AI-generated. Free accounts have daily upload and editing limits; the app shows when it resets.

Tool: Grok

Bring a photo to life: a small smile from someone you miss

Use video mode in the Grok app to make a photo blink and smile, or make a pet turn to look at you.

How to Open the Grok app, tap the input box, choose "Video" mode, switch sound off, tap "+" at the bottom left to add the photo, paste one prompt and tap the arrow at the bottom right. Photo-to-video is a paid-plan feature; what free accounts can do is whatever the app shows.

1 | Natural blink and smile (portrait)

Make the person in the photo move naturally: blink once slowly, a gentle smile at the corners of the mouth, head turning very slightly. Keep the movement small, slow and natural, and keep the original face, hairstyle, clothes and background. Camera stays still. No speaking, the mouth does not open or close. No text.

2 | Pet turns its head (pet)

Make the pet in the photo move naturally: slowly turn its head toward the camera, move its ears gently once, blink once; if the tail is visible, wag it softly twice. Keep the original breed, fur colour, markings and body shape. Keep the movement slow, like at home. Camera stays still. No text.

3 | A light breeze (portrait)

Let a light breeze blow through the photo: hair and clothing edges move gently, leaves or curtains in the background sway slightly; the person keeps the original expression and only blinks once slowly. Keep the original face, clothes, background and lighting. Camera stays still. No speaking. No text.
  • The smaller the movement, the more natural it looks. We only animate the photo: no sound, and no speaking.
  • Before uploading family photos, go to Grok Settings, Data Controls and turn off "Improve the model". That only covers training; the photo is still uploaded to the cloud.

Tool: ChatGPT

ChatGPT image styles: felt, clay, model kit, bobblehead

Upload a character or object photo. Try the short prompt first; paste the long one if it is not close enough.

How to Open ChatGPT, go to Images in the sidebar, tap "+" to upload a character or object photo, and paste a prompt. If it is not right, say "fluffier" or "rounder" in the same chat.

Felt | short

Turn this image into a needle-felted wool style.

Felt | long

Turn the character I uploaded into a handmade needle-felted style: a plush toy made by poking wool with a felting needle, with fine wool fibres and slightly fuzzy edges visible on the surface, soft colours, two small black bead eyes. Place it on a light wooden table, soft natural light, macro photography, shallow depth of field in the background. Keep the original design, colours and features. No text anywhere in the image.

Clay animation | short

Turn this image into a clay stop-motion style.

Clay animation | long

Turn the character I uploaded into a clay stop-motion style: a character shaped bit by bit from clay, with fingerprint marks and the imperfection of hand-moulding, rounded edges, saturated warm colours. Place it in a miniature handmade photo-studio set, soft studio light, macro lens, shallow depth of field. Keep the original design and features. No text anywhere in the image.

Model kit | short

Turn this character into a full plastic model-kit sprue.

Model kit | long

Turn the character I uploaded into a full plastic model-kit sprue: grey-white plastic, the character split into parts such as head, body, limbs and accessories, held to the frame by small gates, with a simple assembly sheet beside it. Place it on a white table, shot from directly above, product-photography quality, even lighting. Only Arabic-numeral part numbers may appear on the parts; no other text and no brand logos. Keep the original design and colour features.

Bobblehead | short

Turn this character into a car dashboard bobblehead.

Bobblehead | long

Turn the character I uploaded into a car dashboard bobblehead: an extra-large head attached to a small body by a spring, head tilted slightly to one side, plastic toy material with a glossy reflective surface. Place it on a car dashboard with soft daylight outside the window, shallow depth of field. Keep the original facial features and colours. No text anywhere in the image.
  • Three things to include: material, detail, and what to keep.
  • For portraits, use only your own photo or one the person agreed to; do not use other people's characters or brand IP. Free accounts have image limits; ChatGPT will tell you when you reach them.

Tool: ChatGPT

Make GIFs in ChatGPT: 6 ideas

Ask ChatGPT for a looping GIF and say what should move.

How to Open ChatGPT and paste a prompt; to use your own picture, tap "+" and upload it first. When it is done, open the GIF to change speed, save or share. If it is off, say "make the movement smaller" in the same chat.

1 | Photo to anime sticker

Turn the photo I uploaded into a looping GIF sticker in Japanese anime style: the person keeps their hairstyle, clothes and general look, blinks once, then waves hello, and finally returns to the starting pose so it loops seamlessly. Change the background to a plain pastel colour. No text and no effect symbols anywhere.

2 | Pet shakes head and claps

Use the pet photo I uploaded to make a looping GIF: it first shakes its head from side to side, then lifts its front paws as if clapping, at a light rhythm, and finally returns to the starting pose so it loops seamlessly. Keep the original fur colour, markings and face shape. Leave the background unchanged. No text and no effect symbols anywhere.

3 | Doodle monster dances

Turn the hand-drawn doodle I uploaded into a moving little monster: keep my lines and colours, like a 5-year-old's scribble with wobbly lines, and have it wiggle its bottom from side to side in a dance, looping seamlessly. Keep the background as white paper. No text and no effect symbols anywhere.

4 | Famous painting parody (public domain only)

I do not have the original image. Redraw an oil-painting version based on the composition of the Mona Lisa (Leonardo da Vinci), then make it a looping GIF: the figure suddenly picks up a cup of bubble tea, takes a sip, looks satisfied, then puts the cup down, looping seamlessly. Keep the oil-painting look. No text and no effect symbols anywhere.

5 | Chibi character sticker

Use the character image I uploaded to make a looping GIF sticker of a chibi version: he makes an OK sign, then nods three times, and finally returns to the starting pose so it loops seamlessly. Full body in frame, plain single-colour background. No text and no effect symbols anywhere.

6 | Old photo smiles again

Turn the old photo I uploaded into a looping GIF: the person slowly blinks once, a gentle smile at the corners of the mouth, then returns to the starting expression, looping seamlessly. Keep the original face, clothes and the grain of the old photo. Camera stays still. No text and no effect symbols anywhere.
  • Use only photos of yourself, your own pet, or people who agreed. For painting parodies pick only public-domain works such as the Mona Lisa, The Starry Night or The Great Wave, never real-person portraits.
  • Three things to state: what moves, what must not change, and no text. OpenAI says the GIF feature is rolling out gradually, so it depends on what you see in your account.
  • Idea inspired by a Xiaohongshu post by Sun Xiang; the images and prompts are Think BIG's own rewrite.

Tool: Suno

Make your own song with Suno: a song for a pet you miss

Advanced mode: title, style and lyrics all written in advance. Every press of Create gives a different result.

How to Log in at suno.com, go to Create and choose Advanced. Paste Title, Style and Lyrics into the matching fields. If you do not like it, press Create again.

A song for a pet you miss (Advanced)

Title: 屁屁，我還記得 (replace 屁屁 with your pet's name)
Style: gentle acoustic ballad, warm fingerpicked guitar, soft piano, female Mandarin vocals, tender and nostalgic, slow tempo, heartfelt, 80 BPM, no heavy drums, no electronic, no rap
Lyrics (Mandarin, replace 屁屁 with your pet's name):
[Verse]
門口的那雙拖鞋 還是被你咬過的樣子
沙發左邊那個位置 到現在我還留著
早上打開門的時候 好像還會聽見
你短短的尾巴 拍著地板說早安

[Chorus]

屁屁 我還記得 你打呼比我還大聲
