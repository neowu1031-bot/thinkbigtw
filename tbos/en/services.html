Services

One assistant, many jobs

Your assistant is assembled from a set of skills scoped to what you actually need. You choose a tier by how many skill combinations you want; we design the mix during installation.

Messaging & chat

Talk to your assistant where you already are — it can live in your messaging apps and reply on your behalf when you want.

Scheduling & reminders

Keep track of appointments, send yourself reminders, and organise your day without juggling five apps.

Research & summaries

Look things up, read long documents, and get the short version — with sources — instead of the whole wall of text.

Drafting & writing

Draft emails, posts, and replies in your own tone. Edit, translate, and polish without starting from a blank page.

Bookkeeping helpers

Log expenses, tidy records, and pull simple month summaries so the numbers aren't a scramble at period end.

Files & documents

Organise, rename, and pull key facts out of your own files — everything stays on your machine.

Customer replies

Handle routine enquiries with consistent, on-brand answers, and hand the tricky ones back to you.

Content & social drafts

Turn a rough idea into a first draft for your channels — you review and approve before anything goes out.

…and a growing set

New skills are added over time. Your tier sets how many skill combinations your assistant can be built from.
