BearSay Mandarin Support

Version 2.0

Current status (2026-11-24): mobile version 1.0 (iOS and Android) has no accounts and no paid features, no analytics and no ads, and does not collect or upload personal data; it does not connect to any AI speech service while running. Accounts on the web version are not live yet. Sections on accounts, sync and payments describe how those features will work once enabled; we will update this page and announce it before enabling them.

Check the questions below first. If you cannot find an answer, write to AI@thinkbigtw.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Frequently asked questions

No sound?

  1. Make sure the device is not muted and media volume is up.
  2. If headphones or a Bluetooth speaker are connected, check whether sound is playing through that device and check its volume.
  3. Demonstration pronunciation is AI-synthesized speech built into the mobile App and needs no network.

How do I use it offline?

  • Mobile release lessons and demonstration audio are included with the App and work offline. "Download this lesson" only copies a bundled lesson to the local cache; no network is needed.
  • Pinned local caches have a limit; at that limit, remove another lesson cache first. This does not remove bundled lessons. The web version needs a connection to load lessons and media from its own host before using offline caches.
  • Progress, coins and pet care are stored on the device and keep accumulating offline.

How do I delete recordings?

  • One recording: tap "Delete recording" in the speaking exercise.
  • All recordings: on the Settings page tap "Withdraw consent and delete recordings". This deletes every recording and withdraws recording consent.
  • Uninstalling the App also deletes them. Recordings exist only on this device and are never uploaded.

How do I turn off reminders?

  • Daily reminder: tap "Turn off reminder" on the Settings page.
  • Festival notifications: use the "Festival notifications" switch on the Settings page; it is independent of the daily reminder.
  • Pet-care notifications are currently inactive: none are scheduled or sent, and there is no separate switch. Pet status appears only inside the App.
  • Streak reminders, which may be scheduled for a known age of 13 or older, are turned off with the daily reminder; children and unknown ages receive none.
  • You can also turn off the App's notification permission in the device's system settings.

Do I need an account?

No. Mobile version 1.0 has no account features, and accounts on the web version are not live yet. Once enabled: you can learn without signing in. Accounts are optional and available only to people aged 18 or older. Settings lets you enter your actual age, stored only on the device; no birth date or identity document is needed. Below 18 or when no age is set, you cannot sign in, receive a sign-in code or sync. The account section says "This feature is currently unavailable" when ineligible. This version has no parental-consent process to open a child account.

How do I sign in and sync?

  1. In a version with account services configured, eligible users can open "Account sign-in" from Settings or their progress page. Enter your email address and then the emailed code. You need to sign in again after closing the App or reloading the web page.
  2. Before the first sync, read the upload scope and tap "Agree and start syncing". Only learning progress and review cards sync; recordings, age, consent records, wallet, paid entitlements, game and pet-care records are not uploaded from the device.
  3. Keep learning locally when offline and retry sync once connected. A version without account services configured explains that progress stays locally; offline learning still works.

How do I stop sync or clear this device?

  • Tap "Withdraw consent and stop syncing" on the account page to stop future uploads and downloads. Data that already reached the server cannot be recalled. Existing cloud data remains until you delete the account.
  • Ordinary "Sign out" keeps local progress.
  • To clear this device, tap "Sign out and clear progress on this device", then "Confirm deletion". Current learning, game and pet-care progress, all local account backups and sync consent records are cleared. Settings, recordings and cloud data remain. Use the separate recording control to delete recordings.

How do I delete my account?

On the account page, tap "Delete account", read what will be removed, then tap "Confirm deletion"; you can also tap "Cancel" at the second step. On success, your cloud email address, account ID, learning progress, review cards and entitlement records are deleted and cannot be recovered. This account's backup, sync consent record and current progress on this device are cleared too. Other accounts' backups, settings and recordings remain. Offline copies on other devices must be cleared separately. Account deletion does not cancel store subscriptions; manage them in the App Store or Google Play. If the connection fails, retry; a failed request does not mean deletion succeeded.

Can I pay on the web?

Mobile version 1.0 has no paid features. The web version does not take payments. If paid features are offered in future, they will direct you to the mobile App; real purchases and restoration use the App Store or Google Play. Store buttons remain unavailable until official store links are provided. There are no ads.

Does it work on tablets?

The first version supports phones only.

Age settings and local restrictions

The age field does not display the account threshold. When ineligible, the account section says "This feature is currently unavailable". This device stores the first reported age and time, and the first reported age below 18 and its time. For 30 days after the first under-18 entry, the age cannot be changed to 18 or above. Leaving it blank, signing out, deleting an account or clearing device progress does not reset these records. Only clearing App data (site data on the web) resets them. These records stay on this device and are not uploaded; this remains a self-reported check.

Mobile version 1.0 (iOS and Android) has no account features; local learning remains available. Accounts on the web version are not live yet.

Account deletion requires email OTP verification within the last 10 minutes. After that, request and verify a new code; refreshing a session does not extend this window. After verification, choose "Delete account" and "Confirm deletion" again. Verification does not automatically delete the account.
