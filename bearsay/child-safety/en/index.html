BearSay Mandarin Child Safety

Version 2.0

Current status (2026-11-24): mobile version 1.0 (iOS and Android) has no accounts and no paid features, no analytics and no ads, and does not collect or upload personal data; it does not connect to any AI speech service while running. Accounts on the web version are not live yet. Sections on accounts, sync and payments describe how those features will work once enabled; we will update this page and announce it before enabling them.

This page is for parents and guardians: what is and is not inside BearSay Mandarin when a child uses it to learn Taiwanese Mandarin, how the content is checked, and how to reach us.

What is not in the App

  • No chat, no comments, no community and no publicly posted user content.
  • No ads. Accounts are available only to people aged 18 or older. Below 18 or with an unknown age, the sign-in entry is hidden, no code is sent and no learning progress is synced. This version has no parental-consent process to open a child account.
  • Mobile version 1.0 has no accounts and no paid features. The web version does not take payments; if payments are offered in future, they will only go through the mobile App's App Store or Google Play. Adults should manage store purchase restrictions and should not share payment credentials or adult accounts with children.
  • When official URLs are configured, Settings has three links: "Privacy policy", "Child safety" and "Support". They open our company website in the system browser; the App has no embedded browser. The website receives ordinary web-request information such as your IP address; the App does not attach progress or recordings.
  • No speech recognition and no pronunciation scoring; speaking practice only records so the child can play it back.
  • Without sign-in, mobile learning progress and recordings stay on the device. When an eligible user signs in, the service processes an email address, account ID and sign-in security records; learning progress and review cards are uploaded only after separate sync consent. Recordings, age and consent records are never uploaded from the device. Support email you choose to send includes your email address and message, kept for 1 year after our last exchange. Please have a parent or guardian write for a child. See the privacy policy at https://thinkbigtw.com/bearsay/privacy/en/.

How the content is checked

Nobody can promise perfection. What we do is review the content one layer at a time:

  1. Structure checks: when each lesson is built, a program checks its format, character forms, syllables and exercise links; an error stops the lesson from moving on.
  2. Safety-word scan: every piece of text in every lesson is scanned against a safety-word list; each hit is read in context by a person and is released only after confirmation, with a record kept.
  3. Two independent reviewers: each lesson is read by two independent review agents; it is accepted only when their conclusions agree, and any disagreement is decided by the person in charge with a written reason.
  4. Sign-off: a lesson is signed off for release only after the steps above; any later edit invalidates the sign-off and the lesson must be reviewed again.

Review points include: sentences suitable for children, no requests for a child's personal information (exercises only ask about fictional characters), an adult present whenever a risky activity appears, no judgement about bodies or money, and no fortune-telling, gambling, alcohol, tobacco or horror elements.

Demonstration pronunciation

Demonstration pronunciation is AI-synthesized speech and is labelled inside the App. Synthesis happens during content production and is reviewed; the App never connects to a speech service while running.

What parents can do

  • Settings lets users report their actual age, stored only on the device, without a birth date or identity documents. The account section says "This feature is currently unavailable" when ineligible. Do not enter an adult age for a child or let a child use an adult account to sync learning. Age is self-reported, not identity-verified; parents should still manage shared devices.
  • Accounts require age 18 or older. Reminder protections separately apply below 13 and to unknown ages: no notifications between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. (daily reminders move to 8 a.m., festival notices go out at 9 a.m.), no pressure-style "your streak is about to end" reminders, and daily reminders use child-appropriate wording only. Users aged 13–17 still cannot use accounts, while reminders retain the existing age-13 threshold.
  • If a child has used an adult account or provided personal information, have a parent write to us. An adult account can use "Withdraw consent and stop syncing" in the App or on the web. To delete existing cloud data, first tap "Delete account", then "Confirm deletion". Stopping sync does not delete cloud data, and deleting an account does not cancel store subscriptions.
  • Recordings can be deleted all at once on the Settings page ("Withdraw consent and delete recordings").
  • Daily reminders and festival notifications can be turned off in Settings. Pet-care notifications are currently inactive: none are scheduled or sent, and there is no separate switch. Pet status appears only inside the App. You can also disable all App notifications in device system settings.
  • For any question, or to report a content issue, write to AI@thinkbigtw.com and we will reply as soon as we can.
