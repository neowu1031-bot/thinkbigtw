BearSay Mandarin Privacy Policy

Version 2.0　Effective date: 2026-11-24

Current status (2026-11-24): mobile version 1.0 (iOS and Android) has no accounts and no paid features, no analytics and no ads, and does not collect or upload personal data; it does not connect to any AI speech service while running. Accounts on the web version are not live yet. Sections on accounts, sync and payments describe how those features will work once enabled; we will update this page and announce it before enabling them.

This policy explains how the BearSay Mandarin app and the BearSay Mandarin web version (together, the "Service") handle data.

Summary:

  • You can use every learning feature without signing in. Without an account, learning progress, recordings and pet-care records stay on your device.
  • Accounts are optional and available only to people aged 18 or older. Eligibility uses the age you report in Settings. Accounts are unavailable below 18 or when no age is set; learning data stays on the device.
  • After you sign in and agree to sync, only "learning progress" and "review cards" are synced from your device to the cloud, together with your email address and a system-generated account ID. Recordings, your age, consent records, coin wallet and paid entitlements are never uploaded from your device.
  • Before the first sync, the App shows what will be uploaded and asks for your consent. You can stop syncing at any time, and you can delete your account directly in the App or on the web; your cloud data is deleted with it.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or rent your data and do not use it for marketing.

2. What we collect and why

2-1. Without an account

The App itself does not collect or upload personal data. Learning features do not connect to analytics, advertising or push servers. Data kept on your device is described in section 3.

2-2. When you create an account and sign in (optional, age 18 or older)

Mobile version 1.0 has no accounts and no paid features; accounts on the web version are not live yet. This section describes how accounts will work once enabled.

An account only lets you continue your own learning across devices (the App and the web). Settings lets you report your age; enter your actual age, without a birth date or identity documents. The age stays on the device and is never uploaded. When ineligible, the account section says "This feature is currently unavailable". Below 18 or with an unknown age, you cannot sign in, receive a sign-in code or sync. You sign in with an email code: you enter your email address, we send you a code, and you enter the code to sign in. There is no password.

While your account exists, we store the following in the cloud:

  • Your email address: used to send sign-in codes and identify your account.
  • Account ID: a random identifier (user ID) generated by the sign-in service, used to link your learning progress to your account.
  • Learning progress: completed lessons and items, experience points, badges, streak and daily goal, streak-freeze weeks, and the time each was last updated.
  • Review cards: each card's next review time, repetition count, mistake count and last-updated time.
  • Sign-in security records: when you sign in, the sign-in service automatically records technical information such as sign-in time and IP address, used to keep accounts secure and prevent abuse.

The following are never uploaded from your device: recordings, the age you set, consent records, coins and wallet, paid entitlements, pet-care and game records, reminder settings, and diagnostic files.

Purposes (specific purposes under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act): 090 consumer and customer management and service; 135 information and communication services; 136 information, communication and database management. We do not use this data for any other purpose, including marketing, advertising or profiling.

Legal bases: creating an account and signing in are needed to provide the cross-device learning you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), performance of our agreement with you); uploading learning progress and review cards starts only after you consent (Art. 6(1)(a), consent); sign-in security records protect accounts and the Service (Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interests).

Providing an email address is optional. Without one you cannot create an account, but all learning features still work and your data stays on your device.

2-3. When you contact us

Only when you choose to write to AI@thinkbigtw.com do we receive your email address and message, including files you attach, such as diagnostics. We use this information only to answer questions, provide support and improve the Service, never for marketing. Our support staff in Taiwan process and store it through the email service we use. We do not disclose it to other third parties except as needed to provide email service or as required by law. We keep it for 1 year after our last exchange, then delete it.

Without an email address we cannot reply. For a child's question, please have a parent or guardian write to us. Do not send identity documents or unrelated personal information.

2-4. Payments

Mobile version 1.0 has no accounts and no paid features; there are no purchases in the App. If payments are offered in future, they can only be made inside the App through Apple App Store or Google Play in-app purchase. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment account details. The web version does not take payments and has no payment features.

3. Data that stays on your device

The following data exists only on your device (for the web version, in your browser's local storage). Apart from the learning progress and review cards you agree to sync under section 2-2, none of it is uploaded:

  • Learning progress, coins and pet-care records.
  • Speaking-practice recordings (created only after you explicitly agree to on-device recording; used only so you can play them back, with no speech recognition, no scoring and no model training).
  • The age you set, and consent records such as recording and sync consent.
  • Daily reminder and festival notification settings.
  • Lesson content included with the App and local lesson caches.
  • A copy of the learning progress for each account that has signed in on this device, so you can keep learning offline.
  • Diagnostic files: created only when you tap "Export diagnostics" yourself, stored on the device and never uploaded automatically; they contain error times, names, messages and stacks, App start timing, and mobile lesson-cache statistics, but no recording files. Error messages may contain runtime environment information, so review the file before sharing it; sharing it with us is your choice.

Sign-in credentials are kept only in memory while the App is running and are not written to device storage; you need to sign in again after closing the App or reloading the web page.

How to delete:

  • On the Settings page, tap "Withdraw consent and delete recordings" to delete all recordings and withdraw recording consent.
  • Choose "Sign out and clear progress on this device", then "Confirm deletion", to clear current learning, game and pet-care progress, all local account progress copies and sync consent records on this device. Settings and recordings remain; use the separate recording control above to delete recordings. Cloud data is not deleted.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all of the above from the device. On the web, clear this site's data in your browser.

4. Sync consent, stopping sync and deleting your account

  • Notice and consent before the first sync: the App displays "With consent, learning progress (completed lessons and items, XP, streaks, daily goal, streak-freeze weeks, badges and update times) and review cards (review schedules, repetition and mistake counts, update times) are uploaded so you can continue learning across devices. Recordings, age, consent records, wallet, paid entitlements, games, pet care, reminder settings and diagnostics are excluded. Withdrawing consent stops future sync; delete your account to remove existing cloud data." Uploading starts only after you tap "Agree and start syncing". The consent record is stored only on your device.
  • Stopping sync: tap "Withdraw consent and stop syncing" at any time in the App or on the web to stop future uploads and downloads. Data that reached the server before withdrawal cannot be recalled. Data already in the cloud stays until you delete your account. To remove cloud data as well, use "Delete account".
  • Deleting your account: on the account page in the App or on the web, first tap "Delete account". The notice reads "Permanently delete your account? Your cloud email address, account ID, learning progress, review cards and entitlement records, plus this account's backup and current progress on this device, will be deleted and cannot be recovered. Store subscriptions are not cancelled; manage them in the app store." Read it, then tap "Confirm deletion" to send the request. You can also tap "Cancel" at the second step. On success, your email address, account ID, learning progress, review cards and entitlement records are deleted from the cloud immediately and cannot be recovered. This account's local progress copy, sync consent record and current device progress are cleared; other accounts' local backups remain. Settings and recordings remain. Offline copies already downloaded on other devices must be cleared on those devices separately. Deleting an account does not cancel app-store subscriptions; manage them in the app store.
  • Signing out: ordinary "Sign out" keeps local progress. To clear it, use "Sign out and clear progress on this device" as described in section 3.

Except as above or as required by law (for example, a lawful request from judicial authorities), we do not provide your personal data to third parties.

6. How long we keep data

  • Email address, account ID, cloud learning progress and review cards: kept until you delete your account.
  • After account deletion: the data above is deleted from the cloud database immediately. Copies may remain in the providers' system backups and are overwritten when the backup cycle ends, within a maximum period we will announce before accounts are enabled (currently not enabled; we will update this policy and announce it before enabling). We do not use or restore those backups during that period except to recover from a system failure.
  • Sign-in security records: currently not enabled; we will update this policy and announce it before enabling (including retention period).
  • Sign-in codes: currently not enabled; we will update this policy and announce it before enabling (including validity period); once enabled, codes expire when used or timed out.
  • Ordinary web-hosting request logs: handled under Cloudflare's log retention settings.
  • Support email: 1 year after our last exchange (see section 2-3).
  • On-device data: under your control, kept until you delete it, clear it when signing out, or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Under Article 3 of Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, you may ask us to let you access or review the personal data we hold about you, give you a copy, supplement or correct it, stop collecting, processing or using it, or delete it. Users in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland also have GDPR rights to restrict processing, to data portability, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting processing before withdrawal), and may complain to their local data protection authority.

The quickest way is to stop syncing or delete your account in the App or on the web. For other requests, write to AI@thinkbigtw.com from your account's email address so we can confirm it is you. We will reply within the period required by law; if the law allows us to refuse, we will tell you why.

8. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only while you hold "Start recording" to record your own speaking practice; recordings stay on this device. The system permission is requested only after you agree to on-device recording.
  • Notifications: used only for the daily reminder and festival notifications you turn on in Settings. Reminders are scheduled locally on the device; there is no push server. For a known age of 13 or older with daily reminders enabled, a streak reminder may also be scheduled when its conditions are met; children and unknown ages receive no streak reminders. Pet-care notifications are currently inactive: none are scheduled or sent, and there is no separate switch. Pet status appears only inside the App. Milestone and return-visit copy is not currently scheduled as system notifications either.
  • Network: learning features in mobile version 1.0 do not need or use the network; only links you open to the privacy policy, child safety or support pages connect, through the system browser. Once accounts are enabled, the network is used when you request a sign-in code, sign in or manage your account. Learning progress and review cards are uploaded and downloaded only after sign-in and sync consent. The web version also connects to load code, lessons and media; links you choose to open connect through the system browser.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device's system settings. The related feature stops; everything else keeps working.

9. Children

The Service is suitable for children learning Mandarin, but accounts are available only to people aged 18 or older. The App uses the age reported in Settings. Below 18 or when no age is set, the sign-in entry is hidden, no code is sent, no sync happens, and learning data stays on the device. The account section says "This feature is currently unavailable" when ineligible. Age stays on the device and is not verified against identity documents. This version has no parental-consent process to open a child account. Reminders separately retain their protections for children under 13 and unknown ages; that threshold differs from the account threshold.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. An account is for the adult's own use: please do not use your account to sync a child's learning or let a child sign in with your account. If you are a parent or guardian and find that your child has created an account or given us personal information, write to AI@thinkbigtw.com and we will delete that account and the related data once confirmed.

The Service has no chat, no community, no user-generated public content and no ads.

10. Third-party libraries and links

The App is built with the open-source frameworks Expo and React Native. We reviewed every library the App uses directly: each is an open-source component under an MIT, BSD or Apache licence, and none of them is an analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDK. The indirect dependencies they bring in were matched by package name against the same categories, with no hits. The full list with versions is kept in our dependency inventory. Account sign-in and sync add no third-party SDK; the App connects directly to the sign-in and database service listed in section 5.

One item deserves a specific note: on Android, the notification component bundles Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging library, so it appears in the Google Play SDK list. The App has no Firebase project configured (there is no google-services configuration file) and never requests a push token, so that library is never initialised and receives or sends no push data; reminders and festival notifications are scheduled locally on the device. The iOS version contains no Firebase.

Mobile release lessons and demonstration audio are included with the App and do not need a content server. "Download this lesson" writes the bundled lesson to a local cache; it is not a network download. If you tap "Privacy policy", "Child safety" or "Support" in Settings, the system browser connects to our website, whose host receives ordinary web-request information such as your IP address. The App does not attach progress or recordings to those links. The web version loads code, lessons and media from its own host, which receives ordinary web-request information; it uses only the browser local storage needed to work and no advertising or tracking cookies. The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play; when you download, install or make an in-app purchase, Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to their services.

Demonstration pronunciation is AI-synthesized speech and is labelled as such inside the App. Synthesis happens during content production; the App does not connect to any speech service while running.

11. Security

Connections between the App, the web version and the sign-in and database services are encrypted with HTTPS. The database enforces access controls so each account can read and write only its own learning progress and cannot read or change anyone else's data. Sign-in credentials are not written to device storage. No system can promise absolute security; if a personal data breach occurs, we will investigate and notify affected users as required by law, including what we are doing about it.
